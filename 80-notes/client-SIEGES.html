Feature note, kept next to the code it describes. Source: docs/notes/client/SIEGES.md

Sieges#

classic MMO-style castle sieges: guilds bid for the right to attack, both sides sign up on rosters, and the battle is fought with siege weapons, forward camps, respawn waves and rings of walls. The castle's owner upgrades it from the territory's treasury. Everything is data, so a new castle, weapon, reward or upgrade needs no code.

(Siege v2, protocol 27. It replaces "Declare siege" from Phase 10. The territory basics—taxes, the treasury, guards, the blessing—are unchanged and are still described in PHASE10.md.)

1. The weekly cycle#

Each territory has one siege slot a week (SiegeDayOfWeek at SiegeHourUtc, UTC). Its cycle goes like this:

Phase When What happens
Peace Most of the week Nothing. The window shows when bidding opens.
Bidding From BidOpenHours until BidCloseHours before the slot (default 72 h → 24 h) Guild leaders bid gold for the attacker slot. The gold is taken at once. A raise pays only the difference.
Preparation From bidding close to the slot The highest bid attacks; on a tie, the earlier bid wins. The winning gold is spent. Every other bid is mailed back. Both sides fill their rosters.
Active SiegeMinutes from the slot The battle (section 2).
(Protection) PeaceHours after a siege No bidding opens during this time. 0 means the next week's slot as usual.

If nobody bids, there is no siege that week.

Safety. Bids, rosters, scores and the castle tier are all saved (shard_state). A restart keeps everything: - A siege due while the server was down starts on restart if there's still time. - Refunds use delivery keys, so a crash or retry can never pay anyone twice. - A bid is only forgotten once its refund is in the mailbox.

2. Playing a siege#

Bidding (guild leaders)#

  1. Open the siege window: the Siege Board, /siege, or Siege... next to the territory in the Faction & PvP window (/faction).
  2. During Bidding, type an amount such as 12g, 12g 50s or 1500s (a plain number means gold) and click Bid.

Limits: - The bid must be at least the territory's minimum (DeclareCostGold). - To take the top spot, you must beat the current top bid by at least SiegeBidOutbidPct (5%). - A guild can bid on one siege at a time. - The castle's own guild can't bid. - Only the leader who placed the bid can raise it.

Joining a side#

Once the attacker is known (Preparation, and during the battle), anyone can click Fight for the attackers or Fight for the defenders:

Who Joins
Members of the attacking guild The attackers, at once
Members of the castle's guild (or, if nobody's guild holds it, the owner faction's players) The defenders, at once
Anyone else (mercenaries) Their request waits until that side's leader (the attacking or owning guild's leader) clicks Accept or Decline

Other rules: - RosterCap limits each side. - Leaders can Remove anyone from their side. - Leave the roster takes you off it. - Requests nobody answered are dropped when the battle starts.

The battle#

  • Lockdown. Only rostered players may be inside the battle area (LockdownRadius round the castle). Anyone else is sent outside every couple of seconds.
  • Sides, not factions. Inside the battle area, the two rosters fight each other whatever their factions, and it's never a crime. You can't hurt your own side.
  • Gates and walls. Attackers break them with Batter, Siege Charges or siege weapons. Defenders Repair them (2 Lumber). Inner rings stay sealed until the outer gates fall (section 5).
  • Siege weapons. Attackers set up Battering Rams and Trebuchets from kits. Defenders man the Castle Cannons. To fire, face the target and use the weapon (section 6).
  • Camps. Attackers raise Siege Camps. Attackers who die come back at the nearest one. Defenders tear them down (section 7).
  • Respawn waves. The dead come back together, every RespawnWaveSeconds. Press Respawn and you'll come back with the next wave. The HUD shows the countdown.
  • Winning. With every gate down, an attacker channels Claim at the Lord's Seal (10 s). Any hit breaks the claim. When the claim completes, the territory, its treasury and its guards go to the attackers. If time runs out, the defenders hold.
  • Score. Kills, damage to structures, broken gates and destroyed camps or weapons all add to your score (section 10). Score decides the bonus rewards.

Afterwards#

  • Everything placed for the siege is removed.
  • Walls are restored.
  • Everyone on a roster gets their side's rewards: gold and items by mail, and honor straight away if they're online (section 8).

2b. Claiming a castle nobody holds (the Lodestone)#

A territory whose castle no guild holds yet isn't besieged. There's no bidding and no siege; it's claimed in a weekly PvP event around its Lodestone. Once a guild holds it, the normal siege cycle (section 1) takes over.

The week of an unclaimed castle (Greywatch's times):

When What happens
Friday 20:00 UTC (2 h before) Crafting opens. A guild leader, and only a leader, crafts the Lodestone pack at the Lodestone Workbench. One pack per guild per event. That guild is now a contender.
Friday 22:00 UTC, 60 min The Lodestone wakes. The whole territory is at war: open PvP for everyone, no crime.
During it Someone carrying a pack, from a contender guild, channels the Lodestone (15 s).
The channel finishes The pack is spent. The territory belongs to the channeller's guild (and their faction): its treasury, guards and taxes. Every member of that guild is mailed the rewards.
Time runs out Nobody claimed it. It stays unclaimed until next week.
After the event Every Lodestone pack left anywhere crumbles. This covers packs on backs, on the ground, and on players who were offline.

22:00 UTC is 6 PM New York time in summer (EDT) and 5 PM in winter (EST). The schedule is stored in UTC, so set ClaimHourUtc to 23 for winter if you want to keep 6 PM.

The pack is a trade pack: - It goes on your back and makes you 30% slower. - No mounts, unless the mount can carry packs. - It drops where you die, and anyone can pick it up. So the fight is over who carries it. - It can't be sold, stowed in a cargo hold, or traded.

Rules of the channel: - One person at a time. While someone channels, nobody else can start. - Any hit breaks it. The territory is told who's channelling, so everyone knows where to go. - Only a contender guild can win. You need the pack on your back, and your guild must have crafted a pack for this event. Anyone can pick up and carry a pack, for example to keep it away from a rival, but a guild that never crafted one can't claim with it. - Uncontested is fine. If only one guild crafted a pack, it can walk in and channel. The PvP is the only thing stopping it.

After the claim: - The siege window shows the normal cycle. - Next week another guild can bid for the attacker slot (section 1) and besiege the new lord. - The new lord inherits the treasury that built up while the castle had no owner.

Setting it up (Content Studio > Factions & PvP > Territories > Lodestone claim):

Field Greywatch Meaning
ClaimPackItemId 7105 The pack. It must be a TradePacks item (ground doodad 150, a MoveSpeedPct, BaseGold 0). Each territory needs its own pack item. 0 = no Lodestone: an unclaimed castle is besieged like any other.
ClaimDayOfWeek, ClaimHourUtc 5, 22 Friday 22:00 UTC.
ClaimMinutes 60 How long the Lodestone is awake (0 = no event).
ClaimCraftHours 2 Leaders can craft from this many hours before, until the event ends.

You also need: - The Lodestone: a doodad with SiegeRole 6 (Lodestone) standing in the territory, with a timed Channel action whose effect is Claim Lodestone (40). Copy Greywatch Lodestone (194) and move its DoodadSpawns row to the heart of the territory. The World log warns at startup if a territory has the event but no Lodestone. - The recipe: a Recipes row that outputs the pack, at any station. The leader-only, one-per-guild and crafting-window rules are enforced by the server for any recipe that makes a territory's Lodestone pack. Greywatch's recipe takes 20 Lumber at the Lodestone Workbench (195) by the Siege Board. - Rewards: LodestoneRewards rows, with LeaderOnly 0 for every member (the leader included) or 1 for the leader only. They're mailed from "Siege Council". Several rows add up. Greywatch's: every member 1g; the leader 5g and 5 Siege Charges.

3. The siege window and HUD#

The window has four parts: - Header: who holds the castle, its tier and treasury, the phase and the times (in your local time). - Bidding: every guild's bid. - Sides: both rosters, with Accept, Decline and Remove buttons for leaders. - The castle: the upgrade tiers, and the Build button for the owner guild's leader.

The window refreshes itself every 10 seconds while open.

The HUD appears at the top centre while you're on a roster near a castle under siege:

Siege of Greywatch  42:13
Outer gates 2/2 down  -  Keep 0/1 down  -  Seal: barred
Attackers 12 (31 kills)  -  Defenders 10 (25 kills)  -  Camps 2
You: attacker  -  score 120  -  4 kills  -  next wave 0:12

Click it to open the siege window.

4. Setting up a territory#

A territory is a Territories row. Content Studio (Ctrl+Alt+E) > Factions & PvP > Territories. The Phase 10 fields (name, zone, owner, tax, castle position, SiegeRadius, schedule, spawns) still apply. The Siege cycle group adds:

Field Greywatch Meaning
DeclareCostGold 1000 Now the lowest bid, in copper (1000 = 10s).
BidOpenHours 72 Bidding opens this many hours before the siege.
BidCloseHours 24 ...and closes this many hours before it. Must be less than BidOpenHours, and open must be under 168.
RosterCap 50 Most players on each side (0 = no limit, max 150).
PeaceHours 0 Protection after a siege (no bidding during it).
LockdownRadius 180 The battle area, in metres round CastleX/Z. 0 = 1.5 × SiegeRadius. Must be at least SiegeRadius.
EjectX, EjectZ 0, 0 Where non-participants are sent. 0, 0 = just outside the area, on their side of the castle.
RespawnWaveSeconds 20 Seconds between respawn waves (0 = respawn at once, max 600).
MaxCamps 3 Attacker camps standing at once (0 = no camps).

Other fields: - AttackerSpawnX/Z is where attackers come back if they have no camp. - DefenderSpawnX/Z is where defenders come back. Castle guards also turn out there. - DeclareMinHours is no longer used.

The server checks every row at startup. A bad one is skipped, with a warning naming it in the World log.

5. Gates, walls and rings#

Castle structures are doodads within SiegeRadius of the castle:

DoodadTemplates field Meaning
SiegeRole 1 Gate, 2 Wall, 3 Seal, 4 Weapon, 5 Camp
MaxHealth Its health (0 = can't be damaged)
BrokenPhaseId The phase it goes to at 0 health (e.g. Breached)
SiegeRing Gates and walls: 1 (or 0) = the outer wall, 2 = the next ring, and so on

Rings. A ring's gates and walls can't be damaged while any gate of a ring outside it still stands.

The seal opens when every gate, in every ring, is down.

So a castle with an outer wall (ring 1) and a keep (ring 2) is fought in three stages: break the outer gates, then the keep gate, then claim the seal.

To add a ring: 1. Make a gate template with SiegeRing 2 (copy Keep Gate, 164). 2. Place it with a DoodadSpawns row inside the castle.

Gates and walls are back to full health at the start and end of every siege.

6. Siege weapons#

A weapon is three things:

  1. A doodad with SiegeRole 4, MaxHealth > 0 (it can be destroyed), PlaceRule 0 (Anywhere), IsPersistent 0, and a PlaceRadius so two weapons can't overlap. - It needs a phase with a Fire action: a DoodadFuncs row whose FuncId is the shared "Fire siege weapon [siege v2]" function (it holds the Siege Fire, 38, effect). - Optionally, a Repair action. - Copy Battering Ram (190) and you get all of this.
  2. A SiegeWeapons row (Factions & PvP > Siege Weapons, or on the doodad's page) saying what a shot does:
Field Ram Trebuchet Cannon Meaning
Side 0 0 1 Who may place and fire it: 0 attackers, 1 defenders, 2 either
StructureDamageMin/Max 900-1200 1500-2200 600-900 Against gates, walls, camps and weapons (0 = never aims at them)
PlayerDamageMin/Max 0 250-400 300-450 Against enemy players (0 = never aims at them)
Range / MinRange 7 / 0 90 / 25 70 / 8 Metres from the weapon
Radius 0 6 4 Splash: everything of the enemy's this close to the impact
CooldownMs 4000 12000 8000 Between shots of this weapon
AmmoItemId, AmmoCount - Trebuchet Stone ×1 - Taken from the shooter's bag per shot
  1. How it gets on the field. Either: - a kit item with a Place Doodad (12) effect (RefId = the doodad) and an ItemUses row with ConsumeOnUse 1. Copy Battering Ram Kit,
    1. Or: - a DoodadSpawns row inside the castle. A world-placed weapon belongs to the castle and is restored after each siege.

Aiming. The shooter faces the target and uses the weapon. A shot goes at the nearest target within SiegeWeaponAimCone (60°) of where they face, between MinRange and Range: - An enemy structure first, if the weapon does structure damage. - Otherwise an enemy player, if it does player damage.

Inner-ring walls aren't targets until their ring opens.

Placing. Weapons can only be placed inside the battle area during the battle, by someone on a roster whose side matches Side. They're removed when the siege ends.

7. Camps#

A Siege Camp is a doodad with SiegeRole 5 and health. Siege Camp (193) has a Tear down action, which is Batter for defenders, and a Repair action. It's placed from the Siege Camp Banner (7103).

Placement rules: - Only by attackers on the roster, during the battle. - At most MaxCamps at once. - At least SiegeCampMinDistance (40 m) from the castle centre.

How it works in battle: - A dead attacker comes back beside the camp nearest to where they died. Without a camp, they come back at AttackerSpawnX/Z. - At 0 health the camp is destroyed, and the destroyer scores SiegeScorePerCamp.

8. Rewards#

SiegeRewards rows (Factions & PvP > Siege Rewards, or on a territory's page) are given to everyone on a roster when a siege ends with a winner. A called-off siege gives nothing.

Field Meaning
TerritoryId 0 = every territory, or just this one
Side 1 attackers, 2 defenders
Won 1 = when that side won, 0 = when it lost
MinScore Only players who scored at least this
ItemId / ItemCount, Gold Mailed (from "Siege Council"), one mail per row. Gold is in copper
Honor Given at once if they're online (not mailed)

A player gets every row that matches, so rows stack. For example, a base reward for everyone on the winning side, plus a bonus row with MinScore 50.

9. Castle upgrades#

CastleUpgrades rows (Factions & PvP > Castle Upgrades, or on a territory's page) are the tiers a castle can buy, numbered 1, 2, 3 and so on, with none missing.

Buying. The owner guild's leader buys the next tier in the siege window, paid from the territory's treasury. You can't buy during the battle. The purchase is saved before it counts.

Effects. Every tier owned adds up:

Field Effect
WallHealthPct Gates and walls get this much more health (+25 = ×1.25)
DefenderStatusId A status defenders have inside the battle area during a siege (refreshed every 2 s, removed when they leave or it ends)
GuardTemplateId, GuardCount NPCs that turn out at the defender spawn when the battle starts and leave when it ends. They attack attackers only; only attackers can attack them

On capture the castle loses CastleTierLostOnCapture tiers (default 1; 0 = keep them all; 99 = lose them all).

10. Rules#

CombatRules (Content Studio > Combat > Combat Rules):

Key Default Meaning
CastleTierLostOnCapture 1 Upgrade tiers lost when a castle changes hands
SiegeScorePerKill 10 Killing someone on the other roster
SiegeScorePerGate 25 Breaking a gate or wall
SiegeScorePerCamp 15 Destroying an enemy camp or weapon
SiegeDamagePerPoint 200 Structure damage per point of score

SiegeChargeRadius 8 (Phase 10) A Siege Charge hits enemy structures this close

12. Testing a siege quickly#

The server needs World:AllowDebugCommands on.

  1. Make a guild (you're its leader) and stand near Greywatch.
  2. /siegenow 1 15 starts a siege of territory 1 in 10 seconds, for 15 minutes. Your guild attacks and you're on its roster. If a siege is already planned, it starts now instead.
  3. /siege opens the window. A second character in the castle's guild (or Greywatch's faction) clicks Fight for the defenders.
  4. Buy a Battering Ram Kit from Hilda. Use it facing a gate inside the battle area, then face the gate and use the ram.

Testing the Lodestone claim: 1. On the Greywatch row, set ClaimDayOfWeek and ClaimHourUtc to a few minutes from now (UTC), and restart. 2. As a guild leader, craft the pack at the Lodestone Workbench (20 Lumber). 3. Channel the Lodestone at (-330, 790) when it wakes.

/siegenow skips all this: it starts a siege even on an unclaimed castle.

Bidding can't be skipped from the client. To test it, set BidOpenHours/BidCloseHours so bidding is open now, restart, and bid. The attacker is chosen within 5 seconds of bidding closing.

13. Troubleshooting#

Symptom Fix
"Bidding for X isn't open." Check the window's times. A bid only works between BidOpen and BidClose. PeaceHours may be protecting it.
Nobody won the bidding Only the castle's own guild bid (it can't attack itself). The bids are refunded once the slot passes.
Refund mail never came The World server couldn't reach the persistence server. It retries every 5 s, and the bid stays saved until the refund is delivered.
Thrown out of the castle area You're not on an accepted roster. Join in the siege window, or wait for the side's leader to accept you.
"The outer gates still stand" Ring rules (section 5). Break every gate of the outer ring first.
"Nothing to aim at" Face the target. It must be between MinRange and Range and within the aim cone. Inner-ring walls aren't targets yet.
Can't place a camp Not an attacker on the roster, too close to the castle (40 m), or MaxCamps camps already stand.
Weapon kit does nothing Only inside the battle area during the battle, and only for the side the SiegeWeapons row allows.
Garrison guards don't appear No tier with GuardTemplateId bought, or the NPC template is missing. Check the World log for a skipped CastleUpgrades row.
Territory missing after editing A bad Territories row is skipped. The World log names the field.
Client and server won't talk Protocol 27: rebuild both Axiom.Network and Axiom.Movement DLLs and copy them to Assets/AxiomMMOKit/Plugins.

14. Reference#

Tables: - Territories (+BidOpenHours, BidCloseHours, RosterCap, PeaceHours, LockdownRadius, EjectX, EjectZ, RespawnWaveSeconds, MaxCamps) - Territories (+ClaimPackItemId, ClaimDayOfWeek, ClaimHourUtc, ClaimMinutes, ClaimCraftHours) and LodestoneRewards: migration 20261012000000_LodestoneClaim and patch 060 - DoodadTemplates (+SiegeRing) - SiegeWeapons, SiegeRewards, CastleUpgrades

All come from the World migration 20261011120000_SiegeV2 and patch 059.

Effect kinds: - 38 Siege Fire: on a weapon's doodad action. - 39 Open Siege Window: RefId = territory, 0 = here. - 40 Claim Lodestone: on a Lodestone's timed action (section 2b). - 28 Declare Siege now also just opens the window. - 24 Batter and 30 Siege Charge now hit any enemy structure, camp or weapon, and respect rings.

Protocol 27:

Opcode Packet
158 C2WSiegeCommand Open, Bid, Apply, Withdraw, Accept, Decline, Kick, Upgrade
