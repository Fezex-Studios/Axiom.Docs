Movement, camera and tab targeting#

How the player moves, how the camera behaves, and how Tab picks targets. Written 10 Oct 2026 for Unity 6.3. The player changes all of this in Options > Controls; this page is what each option does and how the code is put together.

The two control schemes#

Modern (the default)#

Camera-relative, built for tab-target combat.

Input What it does
W / S Move toward / away from where the camera looks
A / D, Q / E Strafe left / right
Hold right mouse The camera turns, and the character turns with it, even standing still
Hold left mouse Look around. Your character keeps running the way it was going until you let go
Both mouse buttons Run forward
` (backquote) Mouse look on/off: the mouse turns the camera without holding a button, and the cursor hides. Opening any window brings the cursor back; closing it resumes
Space Jump
Ctrl (hold) Walk. There's no sprint key: like classic MMOs, you go faster with mounts and speed buffs
Num Lock or Mouse 4 Autorun
Tab / Shift+Tab Next / previous enemy

While moving, the character faces one of two ways (Options > Controls > "While moving"):

  • Face the camera (strafe, backpedal): the default and the one to use for tab-target. The body always points where the camera looks, so A/D sidestep and S backs off slowly. Your target stays in front of you.
  • Face where you run: the character turns toward its direction of travel, and S runs toward the camera. It feels like an action game.

Standing still, the camera orbits freely and the character doesn't spin, so you can look at your gear or read your bags. In combat, with an enemy targeted, the character turns to face it (option "Turn to face your target in combat").

Mounted, you always face where you run, because horses don't strafe. The camera also eases in behind the mount.

Using an ability turns you to face your target instantly, as before, so a target behind you is never a refused cast. In Modern this no longer drags the camera round with you.

Target assist (camera option "Keep my target in view"): in combat, if your target drifts out toward the edge of the screen, the camera eases round until it's back in view. It only does this after you've left the mouse alone for about a second, and never while a button is held. It brings the target back into view; it is not a lock-on.

Classic (character-relative)#

Character-relative. This is how the game worked before.

Input What it does
W / S Along your facing. S is a slower backpedal
A / D Turn (or strafe, with "A/D strafe instead of turning")
Q / E Strafe
Hold right mouse Steer: you snap to the camera's heading, and A/D strafe while it's held
Hold left mouse Look around

The camera eases in behind you as you run.

Options > Controls#

Option Default Notes
Control scheme Modern
While moving (Modern) Face the camera
A/D strafe instead of turning (Classic) Off
Keyboard turn speed (Classic) 180 °/s
Turn to face your target in combat (Modern) On Standing still only, within 40 m
Backpedal speed 0.65 Fraction of run speed when moving against your facing
Mouse sensitivity, invert Y 0.15, off
Keep my target in view (Modern, in combat) On
Over-the-shoulder view in combat On
Screen shake when hit On
Wider view at speed (mounted) On

All of these are saved per PC in PlayerPrefs (MovementSettings, CameraSettings) when you press Apply. Reset controls to defaults puts them back immediately.

Keys are rebindable. Movement keys are in PlayerInputActions. Tab, mouse look and the hotbar are in GameHotkeys.

Tab targeting#

  • Tab picks the next enemy, Shift+Tab the previous one.
  • An enemy is what nameplates colour red: attackable NPCs that aren't friendly to you, and players of a hostile faction.
  • Order: enemies on screen first, nearest and most central first. "A mob 10 m away dead ahead" beats "one 8 m away at the edge of the screen". Pressing again cycles through them.
  • Nothing on screen? It takes the nearest enemy anywhere within 40 m, so Tab always finds the thing hitting you from behind.
  • Clicking a target still works. In mouse look, clicking targets what's under the centre of the screen.

The server still decides what may actually be attacked. Tab only chooses.

Code: Scripts/Gameplay/Combat/TabTargeting.cs. It creates itself at startup; nothing to add to a scene.

Animation for strafing and backpedalling#

Most free animation packs, Quaternius included, only have run-forward clips. Modern strafes and backpedals, so CharacterAnimator covers for it:

  • The visible body turns toward the way it's moving: up to 45° on a diagonal, 90° for a pure sidestep. Your real facing, the one abilities use, doesn't move. Only the mesh does.
  • Moving backwards plays the run cycle in reverse. The controller's Locomotion state uses the LocomotionDirection parameter (1 or -1) as its speed multiplier.

If your controller was made before 10 Oct, select it in the Project window and run Tools > Axiom > Characters & Gear > Animation > Upgrade Selected Animator Controller. It adds LocomotionDirection and wires it, and doesn't touch your clips.

When you get real strafe and backpedal clips later, see CHARACTER_ANIMATION_README.md, "Real strafe clips". You build a 2D blend tree and add a HasStrafeClips bool, and the body-turning switches itself off.

How the code fits together#

PlayerInputActions + MMOCamera     LocalPlayerInput.Gather (every motor step)
        |                                   |
        v                                   |
  InputSnapshot   raw axes, mouse buttons, camera yaw, your yaw, combat target
        |
        v
  IMovementScheme.Build            ModernMovementScheme / ClassicMovementScheme
        |
        v
  MovementIntent  move vector + basis, turn delta, face yaw + rate, carry camera?
        |
        v
  LocalPlayerInput.Resolve         turn -> face -> wish direction -> backpedal
        |
        v
  MoveInput -> CharacterMotor (Axiom.Movement) -> PlayerController -> server
File What's in it
Movement/MovementSettings.cs The options, saved in PlayerPrefs
Movement/Schemes/MovementIntent.cs InputSnapshot, MovementIntent, IMovementScheme
Movement/Schemes/ModernMovementScheme.cs Modern
Movement/Schemes/ClassicMovementScheme.cs Classic
Movement/LocalPlayerInput.cs Snapshot, pick the scheme, Resolve
Scripts/Gameplay/MMOCamera.cs Orbit, zoom, collision, context framing, mouse look, target assist
Scripts/Gameplay/CameraSettings.cs Camera options
Scripts/Gameplay/Combat/TabTargeting.cs Tab

Nothing here changes the server or the protocol. Facing has always been client-chosen and sent with each movement packet. The server still checks speed exactly as before, and a backpedal is only ever slower.

Adding a scheme#

  1. Write a class that implements IMovementScheme. It reads the snapshot and returns an intent; look at the two existing ones.
  2. Add a value to MovementScheme, and a case in LocalPlayerInput.SchemeFor.
  3. Add it to the dropdown in InterfaceWindow.ControlsPage.

The motor, camera and server don't change.

Tuning the camera#

Select Main Camera in main_world and look at MMO Camera in the Inspector.

Field What it does
Pivot Height Where the camera orbits around (head height, from the feet)
Distance, Min/Max Distance, Zoom Step Zoom
Min/Max Pitch How far you can look down and up
Auto Align Speed Classic only: how fast the camera eases in behind you. Set it to 0 for the classic "stays where you put it"
Occlusion Mask What the camera bumps into. Other players and NPCs are always ignored, so someone walking behind you never makes the view lurch
Vertical Follow Speed How stiffly the camera follows steps, slopes and jumps (14 by default; 0 = rigid)
Target Assist Angle / Speed / Delay Target assist (above)
Profiles Per-context framing (Explore, Combat, Mounted, Vehicle, Swimming). Empty = built-in defaults. Add one to override a context's pivot, distance, shoulder offset and FOV

Troubleshooting#

Symptom Cause
The character runs sideways or backwards with the "running forward" animation The controller is missing LocomotionDirection. Run Upgrade Selected Animator Controller
The body twists when strafing and you don't want that You have real strafe clips: add the HasStrafeClips bool (see above)
Pressing ` does nothing A window is open, or you're typing in chat. Mouse look pauses then
The cursor vanished Mouse look is on: press ` again
Tab skips a mob It's friendly to you, dead, further than 40 m, or behind a closer on-screen enemy. Keep pressing to cycle
The camera swings on its own in combat Target assist. Turn off "Keep my target in view"
The camera doesn't follow me round corners (Modern) That's the design: in Modern the camera leads. Hold right mouse, use mouse look, or switch to Classic
