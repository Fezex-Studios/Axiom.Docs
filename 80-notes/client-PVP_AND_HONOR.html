PvP and Honor#

How players fight each other, where, and what it earns them. This page covers PvP rules v2 (12 Oct 2026). Two things from Phase 10 are gone: - the PvP flag (/pvp) - the conflict cycle (tension, war, peace)

Factions, crime, justice and territories are still described in PHASE10.md. Sieges are in SIEGES.md.

1. Zone types#

Every zone has a PvP type, painted with the zone in Tools > Axiom > Dev Tools > Zones:

Type Players vs players Faction NPCs (guards...)
Safe Nobody can attack anybody As always
Peace No PvP at all, bloodlust included Still attack the other faction. Eastreach guards in an Eastreach peace zone still attack Westmark players
Hostile Enemy factions fight freely. Attacking your own faction needs bloodlust, and it's a crime As always; a hostile zone can hold Westmarch, Eastreacher and neutral NPCs
War Anyone, and no crime As always

In the zone tool the types show as Safe, Peace, Hostile and War. In code they're still named Safe, Peaceful, Contested and War. Unpainted ground counts as Hostile.

A spawn point's safe radius is always Safe.

2. Things that change a zone for a while#

  • Festival: a scheduled period, see section 3. No PvP at all, and the zone's faction NPCs turn neutral.
  • A siege or a Lodestone claim event makes its zone War while it lasts. Inside a siege the rosters decide who fights whom (SIEGES.md). Castles and claims work in any zone type, so you can put a castle in a Hostile zone.

3. Festivals#

A festival is a ZoneFestivals row (Content Studio > Factions & PvP > Zone Festivals):

Field Example Meaning
ZoneKey greywatch_1 The zone's key on the zone map
Name Harvest Festival Shown on the HUD: "Greywatch - Harvest Festival 1h 12m"
StartsAtUtc 2026-10-31 22:00 When the first one starts, in UTC (yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm)
DurationMinutes 120 How long each one lasts
RepeatEveryDays 7 0 = once, 7 = every week at that time, 1 = daily

While a festival is on: - Nobody can attack anybody in the zone, bloodlust included. - Every faction NPC is neutral: guards and faction soldiers attack nobody, and nobody can attack them. - Monsters stay hostile. NPCs of the monster faction (an NPC with no faction) still fight. That's how you make an event boss both factions fight together: spawn a monster boss in the zone during the festival, with a loot table for the item. - Everyone in the zone sees "The Harvest Festival has begun in Greywatch - no fighting, and the guards are at peace.", and again when it ends.

A siege or Lodestone event in the same zone overrides a festival.

Testing: /festival ZONE_ID MINUTES starts one now (debug servers only). /festival ZONE_ID 0 ends it.

4. Bloodlust and crime#

There's no PvP flag. Enemy factions (Westmark and Eastreach are hostile to each other) can always fight in Hostile and War zones, and never in Peace, Safe or festival zones.

Bloodlust (/bloodlust, or the button in the Faction & PvP window, /faction) is the only switch. It lets you attack your own faction: - It only works in Hostile and War zones. - In a Hostile zone, attacking or killing your own side gives crime points (and infamy). In War it doesn't. - Too much crime and you're Wanted: guards hunt you, and dying puts you on trial (PHASE10.md).

5. Honor#

You earn honor for killing players of an enemy faction. You won't get it twice for the same victim within HonorRepeatMinutes. Sieges also give honor (SIEGES.md).

Character Info shows, under Reputation: - Honor - Crime, with the Wanted limit (red while you're Wanted) - Infamy, with the Outlaw limit - the Honor Shop button

6. Honor shops#

There are two shops, each selling different items:

Shop Where Sells (as shipped)
Honor Exchange (shop 90010) The Honor Shop button in Character Info, anywhere Supplies and siege gear: Major Health Potions ×5 (20), Siege Charge (50), Trebuchet Stones ×5 (25), Battering Ram Kit (150)
Honor Quartermaster (shop 90011) Varga the Quartermaster, an NPC beside the market vendors Arms and prestige: Superior Iron Sword (600), Amber Pendant (400), Lucky Trinket (800), Griffin Whistle (3,000)

Both are currency shops in src/Axiom.Server.World/Data/currency-shops.json, like the Guild Crest shops. An offer priced in honor has "CurrencyItemId": -1:

{ "OfferId": 1, "ItemId": 45, "CurrencyItemId": -1, "Price": 600, "BundleQuantity": 1, "TabName": "Arms" }
  • Change what they sell: edit their Offers and restart the World server.
  • More honor shops: add more Shops entries.
  • The vendor NPC comes from the Vendors entry with NpcTemplateId 90103. It's created at startup, offset from the market NPCs; OffsetX/Y/Z move it.
  • The button's shop: change shopId in Assets/AxiomMMOKit/Axiom.Client/Resources/UI/Shops/HonorShop.json. A shop the button opens must have "AllowRemote": true.

Buying: the honor is taken in the same save that puts the items in your bag, so it can't be spent twice and can't vanish. The shop window shows your honor balance, and the purchase is refused if you don't have enough.
