Items: Grades, Sockets, Durability and Stack Limits#

Axiom, 12 Oct 2026. Protocol 28. Phase 1 of the "classic MMO-style database" plan.

Before this, every sword of the same kind was identical. Now each copy of an item can be different. That is the base that loot grade rolls, crafting quality and later phases are built on.

A copy carries four things, together called its instance:

Part What it is Stored as
Grade Crude, Basic, Grand ... Mythic. A higher grade means bigger stats and a different name colour. ItemGrades id. 0 means "the item's base grade".
Wear How much durability it has lost. Points lost. Current = Max − Wear.
Gems Up to 3 gems in its sockets. Gem item ids. 0 means an empty socket.
(Binding) Soulbound or not. This existed before; it travels the same way.

The instance goes wherever the item goes: bag, equipment, house coffers, mail, trade, auction house. If you mail a Heroic sword with two rubies, it arrives as a Heroic sword with two rubies.

Every item that existed before this update loads as Basic grade, full durability, empty sockets. You don't need to run anything for that.

1. For players#

Grades#

Id Grade Stats Regrade into it If it fails
1 Crude 90% — —
2 Basic (the default) 100% — —
3 Grand 105% 100% keeps its grade
4 Rare 110% 90% keeps its grade
5 Arcane 116% 75% keeps its grade
6 Heroic 123% 60% 25% chance it drops a grade
7 Unique 131% 45% 35% chance it drops a grade
8 Celestial 140% 35% 5% chance it breaks, 40% it drops a grade
9 Divine 150% 25% 10% breaks, 45% drops
10 Epic 161% 18% 15% breaks, 50% drops
11 Legendary 173% 12% 20% breaks, 50% drops
12 Mythic 186% 7% 25% breaks, 50% drops

These are the starting numbers. Change them in Content Studio > Items > Item Grades.

  • The item's name in its tooltip is shown in its grade colour, and the grade name appears under it.
  • In the bag and the character window, gear of Grand grade or better gets a coloured frame.
  • Gem stats are not scaled by grade. Only the item's own stats are.

The Item Upgrade window#

Open it with /upgrade (also /regrade or /sockets), or right-click a regrade scroll, charm, gem or gem extractor in your bag. The bag opens next to it.

  1. Pick the item. Click one of your worn items along the top, or right-click a piece of equipment in your bag.
  2. Regrade tab. Right-click a Regrade Scroll in your bag. You can also right-click a charm. The window shows: - the chance of success, - what a failure can do, - the gold cost.

The scroll, the charm and the gold are used up whether it works or not. If a failure could break the item or drop its grade, you're asked to confirm first. 3. Sockets tab. Right-click a gem in your bag. It goes into the first empty socket. Remove takes a gem out: - With a Gem Extractor in your bag, the extractor is used up and the gem comes back to you. - Without one, the gem shatters. You're asked before that happens.

You can upgrade worn items too. Your stats update straight away.

Durability#

  • Dying costs every worn item 10% of its maximum durability.
  • Each hit you land has a 3% chance to wear your weapon by 1 point.
  • Each hit you take has a 3% chance to wear one armor piece or your shield by 1 point.
  • At 0 durability the item is broken. It stays on you but gives no stats and no gem bonuses. Its icon turns red, and its tooltip says "broken".
  • Repair at any merchant NPC: the shop window has a Repair All button that shows the price. It repairs everything you're wearing and carrying.
  • Accessories (neck, ring, trinket) don't wear out by default.

Stack limits#

Every item now has a stack limit:

  • Gear: 1.
  • Guild Crests and Aether Crystals: 9,999.
  • Scrolls, charms and gems: 100.
  • Everything else: 1,000 unless its data says otherwise.

Loot, crafting, mail, trade, coffers and shops all fill existing stacks up to the limit, then use new slots. A stack that was already bigger than its limit from before this update is left alone. It just won't take any more.

Where to get the materials#

Hilda's General Goods (shop 1) sells all of these for testing:

Item Id Price
Regrade Scroll (current grade Crude to Rare) 90101 5s
Superior Regrade Scroll (current grade Arcane and up) 90102 50s
Lucky Charm (+10% success) 90103 20s
Guardian Charm (a failure never drops or breaks the item) 90104 1g
Gem Extractor 90105 15s
Lesser Ruby (+3 Strength, weapons only) 90121 10s
Lesser Topaz (+2 Agility, any gear) 90122 10s
Lesser Sapphire (+2 Intelligence, any gear) 90123 10s
Lesser Emerald (+3 Vitality, armor only) 90124 10s
Lesser Pearl (+3 Spirit, accessories only) 90125 10s
Lesser Onyx (+12 Armor, armor or shield) 90126 10s

Later, these move to loot tables and crafting (phases 2 and 3).

2. Testing it quickly#

  1. Log in and get some gold: in the Editor or a development build, press F12 (the admin panel) and add gold.
  2. Buy 3 Regrade Scrolls, a Lucky Charm, 2 Lesser Rubies and a Gem Extractor from Hilda.
  3. Type /upgrade, then click your main-hand weapon in the top row.
  4. Right-click a scroll in your bag, then press Regrade. - Basic to Grand always works (100%). - Open your character window. Attack Power should have gone up by about 5%.
  5. Open the Sockets tab and right-click a Lesser Ruby. Strength should go up by 3.
  6. Press Remove. The extractor is used up and the ruby comes back to your bag.
  7. Die a few times. Then talk to Hilda and check the Repair All price.
  8. Mail the sword to another of your characters, or put it in a house coffer. Hover over it: the grade and gems are still there.

3. For designers: the data#

All of this is in Content Studio under Items.

Items (new column)#

Column Meaning
max_stack How many fit in one slot. 0 = default: 1 for gear, otherwise the ItemDefaultMaxStack rule (1000).

ItemStats (gear; new columns)#

Column Meaning
SocketCount 0–3 gem sockets.
MaxDurability Durability when new. 0 = never wears out.
BaseGrade The grade a new copy has. 0 = the ItemDefaultGrade rule (Basic).
MaxGrade The highest grade it can be regraded to. 0 = the top of the ladder.

Patch 061 set the existing gear like this:

  • Durability: one-handed weapons 100, two-handed 120, shields / chest / legs 90, head / hands / feet 70, accessories 0.
  • Sockets: 1 for Common gear, 2 for Uncommon, 3 for Rare and better.

ItemGrades (new table)#

One row per grade, ordered by Id (a higher Id is a better grade).

Column Meaning
Name, ColorHex Shown in tooltips.
StatPercent Gear stats at this grade (100 = as listed). Whole stats are rounded; percent stats keep one decimal.
RepairPercent Repair price multiplier.
SuccessChance Chance (%) that a regrade into this grade works.
DestroyChance On failure: chance (%) the item is destroyed. This is checked first.
DowngradeChance On failure: chance (%) the item drops one grade. Otherwise it stays as it was.
GoldCost Copper per attempt.

ItemGems (new table)#

Column Meaning
ItemId The gem item.
AllowedSlots Flags, one per equip slot (tick boxes in Content Studio). None ticked = fits any gear.
Stat columns Strength ... Haste: what the gem adds while it's socketed.

ItemUpgradeMaterials (new table)#

Column Meaning
Kind 1 Regrade Scroll, 2 Regrade Charm, 3 Gem Extractor.
MinGrade, MaxGrade Scrolls and charms only work on gear whose current grade is in this range (0 = no limit).
BonusChance Charms: percentage points added to the success chance.
Protects Charms: a failure never destroys or downgrades the item.

Rules (CombatRules)#

Key Default Meaning
ItemDefaultMaxStack 1000 Stack limit when max_stack is 0.
ItemDefaultGrade 2 Grade used when BaseGrade is 0.
DurabilityDeathPct 10 % of max durability lost by each worn item on death.
DurabilityWeaponWearChance 3 % chance per landed hit to wear the weapon 1 point.
DurabilityArmorWearChance 3 % chance per hit taken to wear one armor piece 1 point.
RepairGoldPerPoint 2 Copper per point × (1 + level / 10) × the grade's RepairPercent / 100.

4. For programmers#

The shared type#

Axiom.Network.Shared.Items.ItemInstance is a struct with Grade, Wear, Gem1, Gem2 and Gem3. It also has these helpers:

  • IsPlain
  • GetGem(i) and WithGem(i, id)
  • ==, which stacking uses

ItemDurability has the repair formula. The client and the server both use it, so the price the client shows matches what the server charges.

DTOs that now carry an Inst field:

  • InventorySlotDto, InventorySlotData
  • W2CInventorySlotChangedPacket
  • EquipmentSlotData
  • MailDto, MailEntryData, W2PMailSendRequest
  • AuctionListingDto, AuctionEntryData, W2PAuctionCreateRequest
  • TradeItemData

World server#

Where What it does
InventorySlot.Inst Because it's a struct field, every move, swap, equip and unequip keeps it automatically.
ItemManager Loads grades, gems and materials. Its helpers: MaxStack, Normalize (grade 0 → base grade, clamps wear, drops gems beyond the socket count, keeps non-gear plain), CollectModifiers (grade-scaled stats plus gems, nothing when broken), RepairCost, BuildGradeCatalog.
Core/Inventory/BagMath The one placement rule for everything that writes a bag. CanMerge means same item, same binding, same instance, and the item stacks. Place fills stacks up to the limit, then empty slots. Take takes back exactly one copy (used to undo a failed save).
PlayerManager.TryAddItem(peer, id, qty, inst) / CanAddItem(...) All or nothing, with stack limits. The old 3-argument versions give a new copy.
PlayerManager.TryTakeFromSlot(peer, slot, qty, out InventorySlot taken) Tells you which copy left the bag.
Core/Items/ItemUpgradeService Regrade, socket, unsocket, repair (C2WItemUpgrade 161 → W2CItemUpgradeResult 162), plus durability wear. It's hooked into CombatManager.DeliverHit (wear from hits) and KillPlayer (wear on death).
StatsService.CollectGear Calls ItemManager.CollectModifiers.
Storage, trade, mail, auction Each one carries the instance and undoes the exact copy if a save fails.
Shop exchange Places through BagMath, so purchases respect stack limits.

Persistence server#

  • Columns grade, wear, gem1, gem2, gem3 (int, default 0) on character_inventory, character_equipment, doodad_storage and mail.
  • Migration 20261012120000_ItemInstances, or run SQL/item_instances.sql instead (not both).
  • Items mailed back from a removed house coffer keep their instance.

Auction service#

  • The same five columns on auction_listings and mail_outbox.
  • It adds them by itself on startup if they're missing.
  • The instance follows the item through listing, purchase, cancellation and expiry mail.

Client#

Where What it does
LocalCharacterState.InstanceAt(index) The instance in a bag slot.
GearCatalog Grades, Grade(id), GradeOf, NextGrade, IsGem, UpgradeKind, GemFits, IsBroken, RepairCost, Scaled.
UiItemSlot.Set(..., instance:) Draws the grade frame and the broken tint; the tooltip shows grade, scaled stats, sockets and durability.
ItemUpgradeWindow The upgrade window.
ShopWindow The Repair All button.
InventoryPanelUI Right-clicking an upgrade material or a gem opens the upgrade window.

5. What's next#

  • Phase 2 — grouped loot. Loot groups, weighted picks, always-drop items, and grade rolls: a drop can come out Grand or Rare. That's what TryAddItem(..., inst) is for.
  • Phase 3 — quest types. Craft, use an item, use a skill, enter an area, timed, interact with a doodad; choose-one rewards.

Known limits for now:

  • Crafting and quest turn-ins that consume gear take any copy of that item, not a specific one.
  • The auction "sell" preview icon doesn't show the grade yet. The listing itself does.
