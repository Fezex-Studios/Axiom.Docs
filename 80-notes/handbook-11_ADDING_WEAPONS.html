11. Adding Weapons#

A weapon is an item (guide 10) plus gear stats (what it does), a weapon kind (who can use it, and where it's carried), and a gear visual (its model).

This works the way classic MMOs do it:

Where it's decided You do it
Where the hand, back and hips are on each body: Socket_ points under its bones once per body (Part A)
Where a weapon is carried by its kind: swords on the left hip, staves on the back… (Weapon Sheaths) never: it's automatic (Part C)
How the model sits on those points one rule for every weapon model once per weapon, while modelling (Part B)
Small corrections the gear visual's offsets and Sheathed size rarely

Get Part A right once and every weapon fits every race and gender with its offsets at zero.

Part A: Attachment points on each body (once per body)#

Every body (Human, Elf, Fairy, each male and female) has its own points, because a fairy's hand and back aren't where a human's are.

  1. Dev Tools > Characters & Races > Sockets.
  2. Body: pick one, e.g. Character_Human_Male.
  3. Press Add missing sockets. It adds seven points under the right bones, at sensible starting spots:
Socket Under Used for
MainHand right hand weapons in use
OffHand left hand off-hand weapon, shield in use
Back upper chest two-handers, staves, spears (sheathed)
BackOffHand upper chest a shield on the back
HipLeft / HipRight hips swords, axes, maces, wands (sheathed)
Head head helmets, hats

The hip points hang from the hips, not the thighs, so a sheathed sword doesn't swing with your legs. Every socket also cancels the rig's import scale, so a weapon is its real size even on a 0.01-scale FBX.

  1. Reference weapon: drag in one sword built to the rule in Part B.
  2. Next to MainHand, press Line up. The body appears in the Scene with the sword on that point, and the point is selected.
  3. Move and rotate the socket (not the sword) until the sword sits right in the fist. Then press Save sockets to the prefab.
  4. Do the same for Back and HipLeft / HipRight. Use a shield as the reference for OffHand and BackOffHand, and a helmet for Head.
  5. Repeat for every body.

Fairies: set their Back socket so weapons clear the wings: lower, flatter, or angled away. Or send fairy weapons to the hips. It's only the fairy's socket that changes; the weapons don't.

Part B: The weapon model rule (every weapon)#

Build every weapon prefab the same way:

  • The prefab root is at the centre of the grip, where the fist closes.
  • The blade or shaft points up (+Y) from the root.
  • The edge (or the front) faces forward (+Z).
  • Shields: root at the handle, face pointing forward (+Z), top up (+Y).

How:

  1. Put the FBX in Assets/AxiomMMOKit/Axiom.Client/Art/Weapons/.
  2. Create an empty object named after the weapon (Sword_Iron), drag the model in as a child, and move / rotate the child until the grip is on the root and the blade points up the green (Y) arrow.
  3. Remove colliders. Save it as a prefab.

A model that follows the rule needs no offsets at all.

Part C: Create the item#

  1. Tools > Axiom > Create > Item…, Copy an existing one: a weapon of the same kind.
  2. Details: name, description, icon, category, rarity, level (guide 10).
  3. Gear stats:
Field Example Notes
EquipSlot 6 (Main Hand) 7 for shields and off-hand weapons
WeaponKind 1 (Sword) 1 Sword, 2 Axe, 3 Spear, 4 Mace, 5 Staff, 6 Wand, 7 Shield. Decides who can wield it and where it's carried
TwoHanded off on for greatswords, staves, lances: they go on the back
AttackPower / SpellPower / HealingPower 25 its power
AllowedClasses 0 0 = whoever the kind allows: Warrior sword, axe, spear, mace, shield; Healer mace, staff, wand, shield; Mage staff, wand
  1. Check, Create. Note the item id.

Where each kind is carried (automatic)#

Content Studio > Weapon Sheaths says where each kind goes when sheathed. It starts as:

Kind One-handed Two-handed
Sword HipLeft Back
Axe HipRight Back
Spear Back Back
Mace HipRight Back
Staff Back Back
Wand HipRight HipRight
Shield BackOffHand BackOffHand

An off-hand weapon that isn't a shield takes the mirror spot automatically: left hip ↔ right hip, back → back off-hand. So a second sword doesn't sit on top of the first.

Change a row and every weapon of that kind moves. Each row also has a Sheath Scale for the whole kind.

Part D: The model (Gear Workshop)#

  1. Tools > Axiom > Characters & Gear > Gear Workshop. Pick any race and gender.
  2. Item id: yours, then Load: it reads the weapon kind. Equip slot: Main Hand (Off Hand for shields). Gear prefab: your prefab.
  3. Attach: Socket. Leave In use on (the slot's hand) and Sheathed on (by weapon kind). The line under them says where it will go, e.g. drawn on MainHand, sheathed on HipLeft (from Sword in Weapon Sheaths).
  4. Leave position and rotation at 0.
  5. Preview in Scene. Tick Preview sheathed to see it on the hip or back. Switch the body at the top and Rebuild preview to check other races.
  6. Optional: Sheathed size below 1 (e.g. 0.85) makes a big weapon a little smaller on the back so it doesn't clip. This is the classic worn_scale.
  7. Save for: Everyone → Save to world database.

When to touch the offsets: only for a model that slightly breaks the rule, e.g. a grip a few centimetres off. Classic MMOs do the same: its weapons' offsets are mostly a few centimetres along the handle and a small tilt. If you're rotating by 90°, fix the model (Part B) or the socket (Part A) instead.

Overriding the carry spot for one item: pick a socket in Sheathed instead of (by weapon kind). (stays in hand) means it's never sheathed.

Part E: Send it to clients#

  1. Dev Tools > Overview > Export game data now.
  2. Restart the World server.
  3. Press Play, equip it, draw and sheathe it, on more than one race.

Checklist#

  • [ ] Every body has all seven sockets lined up (Characters & Races > Sockets shows ✓)
  • [ ] Weapon prefab follows the rule: grip at the root, blade +Y, edge +Z
  • [ ] Item has gear stats: slot, weapon kind, two-handed if it is
  • [ ] Gear visual: Socket, automatic sockets, offsets 0
  • [ ] Game data exported, World server restarted, patches committed

If it doesn't work#

Looks like Fix
Every weapon is wrong in the hand the body's MainHand socket: line it up (Part A)
One weapon is wrong, the rest fine that weapon's prefab doesn't follow the rule (Part B)
Right in the hand, wrong on the back the body's Back socket, not the weapon
Right on a human, wrong on an elf or fairy that body's own sockets
Sockets page shows ⚠ bone only press Add missing sockets: without sockets the game uses raw bones, which point in random directions
Swords on the back instead of the hip the item's Sheathed is set to Back; set it to (by weapon kind). Or it's marked TwoHanded
Hip sword swings with the leg an old hip socket under the thigh: delete it and Add missing sockets (they go under the hips)
Tiny or huge weapon the socket was made by hand without cancelling the rig's scale: Reset it on the Sockets page
"You can't use that" the class can't use that weapon kind
