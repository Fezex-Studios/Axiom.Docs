Feature note, kept next to the code it describes. Source: docs/notes/client/SKILL_PLOTS.md

Skill plots: multi-stage skills#

Phase 5 of DATA_PHASES.md (protocol 30). Classic MMOs drive skills with "plots": event trees of delays, conditions, target searches and effects. Here a plot is flattened into ordered steps. That covers combos, cleaves that follow a strike, channelled ticks, chains, cones, lines and projectiles.

How it runs#

A skill with SkillSteps rows does not apply its own Effects when it goes off. The cast, mana, cooldown and range checks are unchanged. Instead a run walks the steps in StepNo order on the shared scheduler. For each step:

  1. Wait DelayMs after the step before it. For the first step this is after the skill goes off.
  2. Check the step's condition. If it fails, jump to ElseStepNo, or skip just this step when ElseStepNo is 0.
  3. Pick targets (TargetMode).
  4. Send W2CSkillStep (opcode 166) to the caster and everyone who can see them.
  5. Land the step's effects on each target. These are Effects rows with OwnerType = 6 (Skill Step) and OwnerId = SkillSteps.Id. If the step has a ProjectileSpeed, they land after the flight time (distance / speed, at most 10 s). Each hit is a normal hit: aggro, combat log, death, quest events.
  6. Repeat Repeat times, RepeatIntervalMs apart (channel ticks), then move to the next step.

A run ends after its last step, or early when: - the caster dies or leaves the world, or - during a Channeled step, the caster is interrupted (Interrupt effect, stun) or moves more than 0.75 m.

Steps already in flight (projectiles) still land. A run is capped at 64 executions. CombatRules.PlotMaxActive (default 5000) caps how many runs a shard has at once.

NPCs use plots exactly like players do: give an NpcAbilities row a skill that has steps.

Target modes#

# Mode Targets
0 Target the skill's target
1 Self the caster
2 Area At Target everyone within Radius of the target (MaxTargets, 0 = no cap)
3 Area At Caster everyone within Radius of the caster
4 Cone within Radius, inside ConeDegrees facing the target
5 Chain the target, then up to ChainJumps more, each within ChainRange of the last
6 Line a strip Radius wide from the caster through the target, the skill's Range long

Hostile skills (by TargetRule) gather enemies; friendly ones gather allies.

Conditions#

# Condition ConditionParam
0 None —
1 Target Has Status status id
2 Caster Has Status status id
3 Target Health Below % percent
4 Caster Health Below % percent
5 Distance Below metres
6 Target Is Player —
7 Chance % percent

ConditionNot inverts the check. Only combat effect kinds are allowed on steps (damage, heal, status, dispel, taunt, interrupt...). Other kinds are refused at load with a warning.

Client#

W2CSkillStep carries CasterId, SkillId, StepNo, TargetIds, FlightMs, AnimationParameter, VfxKey and X/Y/Z (where the step happened).

SkillStepPresenter does three things: - Animation: plays the step's AnimationParameter trigger on the caster if their controller has one, else the skill's normal release, so every step swings at least. - VFX: spawns Resources/Vfx/<VfxKey> at the point for 3 s, if that prefab exists. A missing prefab is fine. - Projectiles: flies Resources/Vfx/<VfxKey>_projectile (or a small sphere) to each target over FlightMs. This matches when the server lands the hit.

Demo content (patch 063)#

Chieftain's Fury (skill 6301) is used by the Warren Chieftain through NpcAbilities 6301:

  1. A strike on his target: 14–20.
  2. 700 ms later, a 90° cleave, 6 m radius, up to 5 targets: 10–14.
  3. 900 ms later, only below 50% health: three channelled spins, 7 m around him, 1 s apart. Each spin does 6–9 and applies Hamstrung.

Animation triggers are Attack and Spin. VFX keys are slash, cleave and whirlwind.

  • Interrupts in KillPlayer, InterruptCast, OnPlayerLeaving
