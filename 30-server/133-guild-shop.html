Server Systems

Guild Shop & Prestige#

Prestige is a guild's shared money. It's earned from missions (mostly group missions, see Guild & Family Progression) and spent three ways:

What Who may spend Where
Guild shop items ranks with Use Guild Shop (GuildPermission.UseShop = 16) Community ▸ Guild ▸ Shop ▸ Items
Temporary guild buffs for every member ranks with Activate Guild Buffs (ActivateBuffs = 32) Shop ▸ Guild Buffs
Changing a group mission the leader Mission ▸ Group Mission ▸ Change Mission

The leader always has every permission; the rest is per rank under Guild ▸ Permissions. New guilds start with Officers having both, Veterans the shop, Members and Recruits neither. Existing guilds keep their ranks; the leader ticks the boxes.

Piece File Job
GuildShopService Core/Social/Missions/GuildShopService.cs Per host: permission check, bag space, buy / refund / activate; sends W2CGuildShopState (190).
MissionRegistry Core/Social/Missions/MissionRegistry.cs Prestige, the guild's weekly sales, each character's weekly purchases, timed buffs and the guild log; saved with the mission state. Timed buffs feed BuffPct.
MissionCatalog Core/Social/Missions/MissionCatalog.cs Loads GuildShopItems and GuildBuffOffers.
C2WGuildShopHandler Core/Packets/C2WHandlers/C2WMissionHandlers.cs C2WGuildShop (189): GuildShopAction Refresh, Buy, Activate.
GuildManager.HasPermission Core/Social/GuildManager.cs Rank permission check (leader = all).
Client GuildShopState.cs, UiCommunityView.cs (Shop tab), OrganizationAuras.cs, Art/Resources/UI/Social/GuildShop.uss Items, Guild Buffs and Log pages; timed buffs on the buff bar.

Shop items#

GuildShopItems (Content Studio ▸ Quests & Progression ▸ Guild Shop Items): ItemId × Count per purchase, Price (Prestige), MinGuildLevel (locked before), MemberWeeklyLimit (per character, follows them out of the guild; 0 = none), GuildWeeklyStock (whole guild; 0 = none), Category and SortOrder (filter chips, order), Enabled.

Limits reset with the weekly mission reset (MissionWeeklyResetDay, MissionResetHourUtc). A purchase checks bag space first; if the items still don't fit, the Prestige and the limits are given back (a refund entry in the log). The seed has 15 items, up to a Griffin Whistle at guild level 10.

Temporary guild buffs#

GuildBuffOffers: Name, Description, Kind (the six buff kinds), Value (%), DurationMinutes, Price, MinGuildLevel, IconName.

Activating one gives every member the buff for its duration, on any zone host, including members who log in later. It adds to the guild's level buffs; only one buff of each kind runs at a time. Members get a chat notice, and it shows with a timer on everyone's buff bar, on Manage ▸ Guild Buffs and under Shop ▸ Guild Buffs. The seed has seven offers (Guild Rally +10% XP 1 h … Grand Rally +20% XP 2 h from level 8).

Guild log#

Shop ▸ Log shows the last 50 entries: purchases, refunds, buff activations, group mission changes and Prestige earned from group missions.

Rebuild Persistence after changing permission bits#

Persistence saves rank permissions with & GuildPermission.All. All is now 63; an old Persistence build still masks with 15 and the two new checkboxes don't stick. Rebuild and redeploy Persistence whenever GuildPermission changes.

Rule GuildShopEnabled (default 1) set to 0 closes both the shop and the timed buffs. Packets: 189, 190. Feature note: Guild shop.

Recipes#

Content: sell a new item#

Content Studio ▸ Guild Shop Items ▸ add a row: the item, price, the level it unlocks at and the weekly limits. Restart the World server.

Content: a new timed buff#

Content Studio ▸ Guild Buff Offers ▸ add a row with a Kind (1-6), Value, DurationMinutes and Price. A new kind needs code: see a new buff kind.

Code: a new guild permission#

  1. Add the bit (64) to GuildPermission in src/Shared/Axiom.Network/Shared/Social/SocialTypes.cs and add it to All.
  2. Decide the defaults for new guilds' ranks there too.
  3. Check it with GuildManager.HasPermission(session, GuildPermission.X, out var guild, out var refused) where the action happens.
  4. Client: a toggle on the Permissions page in UiCommunityView.
  5. Rebuild and redeploy Persistence (it masks with All).
