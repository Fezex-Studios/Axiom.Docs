Feature note, kept next to the code it describes. Source: docs/notes/client/DUNGEONS.md

Dungeons: end to end#

How to make an instanced dungeon in Axiom, from an empty patch of world to a run with mobs, a boss, a way out, a time limit and a daily entry limit. Everything about a dungeon is data, so a dungeon you can run forever is the same dungeon with two numbers set to 0.

Read section 1 once. Then use section 2 (the Create wizard: about ten minutes) or section 3 (the same thing by hand, if you want to know what every row is).

Contents#

1. How dungeons work#

A dungeon is a place, built once. Its rooms are ordinary world space: a region far away from the open world that nobody can walk to. The two that ship with Axiom are at tile 32,32 (The Warrens) and 33,32 (The Sunken Crypt), about 16 km from the open world.

Every group gets its own copy of what's in it. When a party goes in, the server spawns the dungeon's mobs, boss and objects into a private instance: an interest layer only that group can see. Another party walking in at the same moment gets a different instance with its own mobs. This is what lets a dungeon be "cleared": mobs killed in your instance never come back in it.

 open world                                dungeon region (far away)
 ┌──────────────────────┐                 ┌───────────────────────────────┐
 │  [Entrance portal] ──┼── Enter ──────► │ Entry point                   │
 │         ▲            │                 │   mobs ... mobs ... BOSS      │
 │       Exit point ◄───┼── Leave ─────── │ [Exit portal]  (or the        │
 └──────────────────────┘                 │  tracker's Leave button)      │
                                          └───────────────────────────────┘
                                   party A sees instance #1, party B #2 ...

The pieces, and the table each lives in:

Piece Where it lives
The dungeon: name, level, party size, region, entry/exit points, run rules Dungeons (one row)
Its mobs and boss NpcSpawners rows with DungeonId = the dungeon
Which of those is a boss the NPC's Rank = Boss (NpcTemplates)
Chests, the exit portal, anything else inside DoodadSpawns rows with DungeonId = the dungeon
The entrance a portal doodad in the open world (DoodadSpawns, DungeonId 0) whose action has an Enter Dungeon effect pointing at the dungeon
What players stand on tile scenes for the region, or a prefab (ClientKey), or nothing (a plain floor at FloorY)

A run, from the player's side:

  1. Walk up to the entrance portal and press F (Enter).
  2. The server checks level, party size and entry limit, then puts you in your party's instance (or a new one) at the entry point.
  3. A tracker at the top of the screen shows the dungeon's name, Bosses 0/1, the clock if there's a time limit, and 2 of 3 runs left if runs are limited.
  4. Kill the boss (every boss, if there are several): "… cleared!".
  5. Leave through the exit portal or the tracker's Leave dungeon button, or get sent out automatically (CompleteExitSeconds), or run out of time (the run fails and you're sent out).
  6. You come out at the dungeon's exit point in the open world.

Dying inside brings you back at the entry point, inside. Logging out inside and back in puts you back in your instance if it's still open, otherwise at the exit. An instance with nobody in it closes after EmptySeconds (5 minutes by default).

Your mount stays outside (ground mounts can be allowed with AllowMounts; flying is never allowed inside). Your pet comes with you.

2. Quick start: the Dungeon wizard#

Tools > Axiom > Create > Dungeon… (or Hub > Create > Dungeon).

  1. Start. Copy The Warrens (suggested). The copy gets the same rules and region size, and is moved to the next free region automatically (two dungeons can't share space). Its mobs and portals are not copied: every dungeon places its own.
  2. Details. Name, description, minimum level, party size, and the run rules: runs per day, time limit, how long to loot after clearing, mounts (section 4 explains each; hover a field for a tooltip).
  3. Where it is. The region (MinX…MaxZ), the floor height, and an optional ClientKey prefab. Leave them as the wizard set them for a first dungeon.
  4. Entry and exit. - Entry: where players appear inside. The wizard puts it near the region's corner; fine for now. - Exit: where players come out in the open world. Fly the Scene view to where your entrance will be (e.g. a cave mouth) and press Exit = where the Scene view is looking.
  5. Mobs and bosses. Press Add a spawner near the entry a few times. Each row is a spawner: set its TemplateId to the NPC (click the field and type a name), Count to how many, Radius to how far apart. Add one spawner whose NPC is a boss (an NPC with Rank = Boss, e.g. Warren Chieftain). The card says "1 boss spawner(s)" when you've got one.
  6. The way in and the way out. - Fly the Scene view to the cave mouth again and press Make an entrance portal and place it where the Scene view is looking. This copies an existing entrance, gives the copy its own action and points it at this dungeon. - Press Add an exit … near the entry for the exit portal inside.
  7. Check, then Create. One patch, written to the database.
  8. Restart the World server. Walk to your entrance, press F.

That's a working dungeon. It has no art yet: you stand on a plain dark floor. Section 3.2 is how to build the real thing.

3. Step by step, by hand#

Everything the wizard does, as rows, so you can edit an existing dungeon in Content Studio (Ctrl+Alt+E) or understand what the wizard made.

3.1 The Dungeons row#

Content Studio > World > Dungeons > new row (or Duplicate one).

Field Example Notes
Name The Hollow Mine Shown on entering and on the tracker
Description Abandoned tunnels...
MinLevel 10 Lower levels are refused at the door
MaxPlayers 5 Players inside one instance at once
MinX, MinZ, MaxX, MaxZ 17408, 16384, 17920, 16896 The region. Must not overlap another dungeon's. Tile-aligned (multiples of 512) keeps it simple
FloorY 0 Height of the placeholder floor (when there's no geometry)
ClientKey (empty) A prefab key for the dungeon's geometry, if it's one prefab
EntryX, EntryY, EntryZ, EntryYaw 17424, 0, 16400, 0 Inside the region
ExitX, ExitY, ExitZ -640, 12, 760 In the open world, outside every region
Run rules Section 4

The server checks every row when it starts. A row with a problem (entry outside the region, exit inside it, overlapping another dungeon…) is skipped with a warning in the World server's log naming the problem.

3.2 The geometry: what players stand on#

Three options, in the order the client tries them:

  1. Tile scenes (the real way). The region is ordinary 512 m tiles, so Dev Tools > World Tiles creates them like any other: create the tile(s) covering the region (e.g. 34,32), build the dungeon in them (terrain, or a modelled floor from Art/Dungeons/ kit pieces with mesh colliders), export the server collision (Dev Tools > World Tiles > Server collision) and add them to Build Settings. The client streams them like the open world. WORLD_BUILDING_GUIDE.md has the details.
  2. One prefab: put its key in ClientKey. It's placed with its pivot at the region's min corner (MinX, FloorY, MinZ).
  3. Nothing: a plain dark slab across the whole region, its top at FloorY. Enough to test mobs, bosses and rules before there's any art.

The server only knows heights from exported heightmaps. A dungeon floor at one height (FloorY, and spawners at that Y) is the simplest thing that works everywhere.

3.3 Mobs#

NpcSpawners rows with DungeonId = your dungeon:

Field Meaning
TemplateId The NPC (NpcTemplates)
X, Y, Z Where, inside the region. Y = the floor height
Count How many of that NPC, every run
Radius How far from X/Z they're spread
DungeonId This dungeon. 0 would put them in the open world

Inside an instance, killed NPCs never respawn: cleared stays cleared. Use Dev Tools > World Map to place spawners visually in the dungeon's tile scene, then set their DungeonId in Content Studio, or use the wizard's buttons.

3.4 The boss#

A boss is any NPC whose Rank is Boss (NpcTemplates.Rank = 2). Make one with Tools > Axiom > Create > NPC…: copy Warren Chieftain and change it, or set Rank on your own NPC. Then a spawner for it inside the dungeon (3.3).

  • Every boss in a run must die for it to be cleared. Two boss spawners = two bosses = Bosses 0/2 on the tracker.
  • Bosses ignore stuns, roots and silences (CombatRules BossCrowdControlImmune).
  • Boss phases (NpcPhases): at a health %, it yells, summons adds (SummonTemplateId), switches abilities. Content Studio > NPC > Boss phases.
  • Loot: give the boss a loot table (LootTableId), and/or put a chest next to it (a DoodadSpawns row with DungeonId, like Chieftain's Hoard).

A dungeon with no boss works, but can never be cleared (no "cleared" message, no CompleteExitSeconds, and CountEntryOn = 1 never counts).

3.5 The entrance (open world)#

A portal doodad in the open world whose F action has an Enter Dungeon effect with RefId = the dungeon's Id. Each dungeon needs its own entrance template, because the dungeon id is part of it:

  1. Content Studio > Doodads > Warren Entrance > Duplicate (with children). Rename it.
  2. Open its phase > its action (DoodadFuncs) > its function (FuncId). If that function is shared with the original, Duplicate the function too and point the action at the copy.
  3. In the function's Effects, the row with Kind Enter Dungeon: set RefId to your dungeon's Id.
  4. Place it: Dev Tools > Doodads, pick your entrance, click the ground by your exit point. (DungeonId 0: it's in the open world.)

The wizard's Make an entrance portal… button does all four.

3.6 The exit (inside)#

Any doodad whose action has a Leave Dungeon effect. It needs no dungeon id, so one exit template works for every dungeon: Way Out. Place a DoodadSpawns row for it inside your region with DungeonId = your dungeon (Dev Tools > Doodads in the dungeon's tile, then set DungeonId; or the wizard's button).

Players can also always leave with the tracker's Leave dungeon button (click twice), which does the same as the exit portal.

3.7 Commit and restart#

Commit (Ctrl+S in Content Studio, or Create in the wizard), then restart the World server: it reads dungeons, spawners and doodads once, at startup.

4. Run rules: limits, timer, clearing#

All on the Dungeons row (Content Studio > Dungeons > Runs group).

Entry limit: runs per day (or week)#

Column Meaning
EntryLimit Runs one character may start per period. 0 = unlimited
EntryLimitPeriodHours The period: 24 = daily, 168 = weekly (0 = 24)
CountEntryOn 0 = a run counts when you enter it. 1 = when you clear it (every boss dead): walking in and out is free

Rules:

  • Going back into your own run (you left, died, logged out) never counts again. Only starting a new run, or joining a party member's run you weren't part of, does.
  • Counts are per character and saved with the character, so logging out or restarting the server doesn't reset them.
  • Every character's counts reset at the same moment: daily at CombatRules DungeonResetHourUtc (0 = midnight UTC), weekly on Monday at that hour. Not 24 hours after your own first run.
  • At the limit, the entrance refuses: "You've used all 3 runs of The Warrens - more in 5h 12m." The tracker shows "2 of 3 runs left - resets Tue 00:00" while you're inside.

Time limit#

Column Meaning
TimeLimitSeconds Clear it within this long. 0 = no time limit
  • The clock starts when the run opens (the first player in), not per player.
  • A warning banner comes CombatRules DungeonTimeWarningSeconds before the end (60 by default; 0 = none). The tracker's clock turns red in the last minute.
  • Time up, not cleared: the run fails. Everyone inside is sent to the exit and the instance closes. Going back in starts a new run (and counts against the limit).
  • Clearing it stops the clock.

When it's cleared#

Column Meaning
CompleteExitSeconds After the last boss dies, everyone still inside is sent out after this long (time to loot), and the run closes. 0 = stay until you leave

Other#

Column Meaning
AllowMounts Ground mounts may be summoned inside. Flying is never allowed in a dungeon
EmptySeconds An instance with nobody inside closes after this long (0 = CombatRules DungeonEmptySeconds, 300). Members can come back to it until then
MaxPlayers Players inside one instance at once
MinLevel Level needed to enter

5. Recipes#

A dungeon you can run forever (practice, farming, testing): EntryLimit 0, TimeLimitSeconds 0. That's it.

Three runs a day, like a classic MMO daily: EntryLimit 3, EntryLimitPeriodHours 24, CountEntryOn 0.

One clear a week, but you can wipe and retry freely (raid lockout): EntryLimit 1, EntryLimitPeriodHours 168, CountEntryOn 1.

Time trial: TimeLimitSeconds 900 (15 minutes), CompleteExitSeconds 30. Clear it in time or be sent out.

Kick everyone out a minute after the boss dies (loot, then go): CompleteExitSeconds 60.

Ride through a big open cavern: AllowMounts 1.

Reset at 6 am server time (UTC+0): CombatRules DungeonResetHourUtc 6.

What ships in patch 058:

Dungeon Rules
The Warrens 3 runs a day, counted on entering; sent out 60 s after the Chieftain dies
The Sunken Crypt Unlimited runs; 20-minute time limit

6. Testing checklist#

  • [ ] The World server's log has no [Instances] Dungeon N ... skipped line for your dungeon.
  • [ ] The entrance is in the open world and F says Enter.
  • [ ] Entering puts you at the entry point, on a floor (tiles, prefab or the placeholder slab), and the tracker shows the name.
  • [ ] Bosses 0/N on the tracker matches the boss spawners you placed.
  • [ ] Killing the boss shows "… cleared!".
  • [ ] The exit portal and the tracker's Leave button both put you at the exit point.
  • [ ] Entry limit: run it EntryLimit times; the next try is refused with the reset time.
  • [ ] Time limit: set TimeLimitSeconds 60 to test, wait it out: warning, then "Time's up" and you're outside.
  • [ ] Two parties entering at once see different mobs.

To test quickly, set the limit and timer low, restart, test, then set them back. Your own counts are in the persistence database (character_dungeon_entries); delete your rows there to reset them.

7. Troubleshooting#

Symptom Cause / fix
"That leads nowhere." at the entrance The entrance's Enter Dungeon effect's RefId isn't a loaded dungeon: wrong id, or the dungeon row was skipped (check the World server's log)
Log: "the entry point is outside its region" Entry X/Z must be between Min and Max
Log: "the exit point is inside its region" The exit must be in the open world
Log: "its region overlaps dungeon N's" Move it: every dungeon needs its own region
I fall forever after entering No floor at the entry: build the tile scene, or check FloorY and EntryY match (placeholder floor's top is at FloorY)
No mobs inside Their spawners' DungeonId isn't the dungeon's Id (0 = open world), or they're outside the region
Tracker shows no "Bosses" No NPC with Rank = Boss in its spawners
"You've used all N runs" but I haven't Joining a party member's run you weren't in counts as a run. Counts reset at DungeonResetHourUtc
Counts reset when the server restarts The persistence server is missing the character_dungeon_entries table: run its migration (see the update notes)
My pet didn't come in Pets follow on entering; if it was dismissed by dying, summon it again
Can't summon my mount inside AllowMounts is off (the default). Flying mounts never fly inside

For programmers#

  • Server: Core/Instances/InstanceManager.cs (runs, limits, timer, bosses via CombatManager.NpcDied), entry counts in DungeonEntryComponent, saved in the character's full save (DungeonEntries), persistence table character_dungeon_entries.

  • Client: InstanceState (state), DungeonTrackerUI (the tracker), InstanceFloor (the placeholder floor / prefab).
