Mounts and Pets#

Ground mounts, flying mounts, and pets that give their owner a buff. All of it is data: a new mount is an item and a Mounts row, a new pet is an item, an NPC and a PetBuffs row. No code.

1. Playing: controls#

Mounts. Use the mount's item (a whistle) to get on; use it again to get off. Getting hit, attacking, harvesting or dying throws you off.

Flying mounts:

Do Keys
Take off Jump and keep holding Space
Climb Hold Space
Dive Hold X
Fly where you look Hold the right mouse button (or mouse look) and move forward: look up to climb, down to dive. A normal over-the-shoulder camera flies level
Land Touch the ground without holding Space

Space and X are the Swim Up / Swim Down keys (rebindable in Settings > Controls). Get off in the air and you fall. Flying doesn't work inside a dungeon.

Water. Ride into deep water and the mount swims on the surface with you still seated. Swim Down (X) does nothing while mounted, so the mount never dives; get off to dive. (The server sets this with the mount state, MovementProfile.SurfaceSwimOnly, protocol 45.)

Pets. Use the pet's item to summon it; use it again to send it away. While it's out you have its buff (on your buff bar, with no timer). It follows you everywhere, into dungeons too, and goes away when you die or log out. Ride or fly off and it falls behind; once it's more than 30 m away it blinks to just behind you (on the ground under you if you're flying).

2. Making a mount#

A mount is three rows. Content Studio (Ctrl+Alt+E):

  1. Mounts (NPCs category) > new row:
Field Example Meaning
Name Swift Stag
ModelType Mount_Stag The prefab key (section 4)
SpeedMultiplier 1.8 Ground speed relative to running (1-4). The server's speed check widens by exactly this
CanCarryPack 0 1 = may be ridden with a trade pack on your back
  1. Items > new row: the whistle. Name, icon, category, rarity.
  2. On that item: an Effects row (Owner = the item) with Kind = Mount and RefId = the mount's Id, and an ItemUses row for its cooldown (EffectType 4, EffectValue = mount Id, CooldownMs 2000, ConsumeOnUse 0).

The quickest way: Tools > Axiom > Create > Item…, copy Horse Whistle, rename it, and change the mount id in On use (EffectValue) and in its Effects row (RefId).

  1. Commit, restart the World server, and Update Game Data (Hub > Tools) so the whistle's name and icon show in game.

3. Making it fly#

On the Mounts row, the Flight group:

Field Example Meaning
CanFly 1 This mount flies
FlySpeedMultiplier 2.4 In the air: speed relative to running on foot (1-5). 0 = the same as SpeedMultiplier
FlyVerticalSpeed 7 Climb / dive speed in m/s on Space / X (1-30). 0 = 6

That's all. The server tells the rider's client it may fly, and its movement check allows exactly that speed and climb, no more: a flying mount can't be used as a speed hack, and flight can't be faked without one.

Flight is switched off: - inside a dungeon (always); - the moment you're off the mount (you fall).

4. Mount models and animation#

The model. Name the FBX after the mount's ModelType (the Field Horse is Mount_Horse, so Mount_Horse.fbx) and put it in Assets/AxiomMMOKit/Axiom.Client/Art/Mounts/. That folder imports it with a Generic rig and its clips, loops the idle/walk/run ones and keeps them in place. Select it and run Tools > Axiom > Art & Assets > Make World Prefab From Selected Model: it makes Art/Objects/Mounts/<Key>.prefab with a RiderSeat child and registers the key in the WorldObjectPrefabs catalog (Art & Assets > Select World Prefab Catalog shows every key).

If the key is already in the catalog (an older placeholder prefab), the new prefab is still made but the entry is left pointing at the old one: drag the new prefab onto that entry's Prefab slot and save the project. Or empty the slot first and the tool fills it.

Size. Before making the prefab, drag the FBX into a scene next to a character: a horse's back should be about chest height. Model packs exported from Blender often import several times too big or too small; fix it with Scale Factor on the FBX's Model tab (the Quaternius horse needs about 0.25-0.3). If the prefab already exists, delete it and its controller and make them again (the prefab's ground offset and RiderSeat were measured at the old size).

Where the rider sits. The game puts the rider's feet (the body's pivot) on the prefab's RiderSeat child, not their hips. So with a sitting pose the seat goes below the saddle, by about the pose's hip height: for a Mixamo sitting pose with the hips 60 cm above the feet, put RiderSeat about 0.6 m under the top of the back, then fine-tune in small steps. Forward/back, just in front of the middle of the back. Without a RiderSeat the rider stands at the mount's pivot.

Which way it faces. The mount prefab is lined up with the rider, so the horse's head must point along the prefab root's blue (Z) arrow. To check, ride forward (W): the camera should see the rider's back and the horse's tail. Whichever of the two faces the camera is the one that's backwards:

  • The horse is backwards: open the prefab, click the model child (the one with the Animator, not the root) and set Rotation Y to 180. Then negate its Position X and Z (Make World Prefab moved the model to centre it; turning it flips that offset too, and the horse ends up shifted towards its tail). Move RiderSeat forward again if needed.
  • The rider is backwards, only while mounted: it's the riding clip. Unity guessed the clip's facing 180° wrong (easy with a sitting pose). Select the clip's FBX, Animation tab, Root Transform Rotation: tick Bake Into Pose, Offset 180, Apply. To be sure, take the clip out of the Ride state: if the rider then faces forward, the clip was the cause.

The mount's own animation. If the mount prefab has an Animator whose controller declares any of these parameters, the game sets them every frame (any it doesn't declare are skipped):

Parameter Type Value
Speed float Horizontal speed, m/s
VerticalSpeed float Up/down speed, m/s
Grounded bool On the ground
Flying bool In the air on a flying mount
Jump trigger The rider jumped (the rider stays in its Ride pose; the mount jumps)
Swimming bool In deep water (the mount swims on the surface; the rider stays in its Ride pose)

Tools > Axiom > Characters & Gear > Animation > Create Mount Animator Controller builds one for you. Select the mount's prefab (or its FBX), pick its SpeedMultiplier, and it makes Art/Animations/Controllers/<Key>_Mount.controller with a blend tree on Speed from the clips it finds by name, then puts it on the prefab's Animator with root motion off:

Speed Clip found by name
0 Idle
1.25 m/s WalkSlow / Slow
2.5 m/s (walking) Walk
5 m/s x SpeedMultiplier (9 for the Field Horse) Gallop / Run / Canter / Trot
Jump trigger (back to Locomotion on landing) Jump / Leap
Swimming bool Swim / Paddle (without one, Locomotion keeps playing in water)
Flying bool Fly / Flap (flying mounts)

If the legs move faster or slower than the mount travels, open the controller, double-click Locomotion and change the Run threshold. Building one by hand works the same way: a blend tree on Speed, and a Flying bool for a flying state. Turn Apply Root Motion off on the mount's Animator (the game does too).

Example: a horse from a model pack. Quaternius' Farm Animals horse (Idle, WalkSlow, Walk, Run, Jump, Death):

  1. Rename Horse.fbx to Mount_Horse.fbx and drop it in Art/Mounts.
  2. Scale Factor about 0.25 (it imports roughly four times too big).
  3. Make World Prefab (if Mount_Horse already points at an old placeholder, drag the new prefab onto that catalog entry).
  4. Create Mount Animator Controller, 1.8.
  5. Give the rider a riding clip (below), then place RiderSeat about 0.6 m under the back, just forward of the middle.
  6. Play, use the Horse Whistle, ride forward and check which way both face.

The horse faces the right way as imported. Jump and Death aren't used by the game.

The rider's animation. The character controller has Mounted (true while riding) and Flying (true in the air on a flying mount, if you want a different pose). Controllers made with Create Character Animator Controller have a Ride state that plays while Mounted; for an older one, select it and run Tools > Axiom > Characters & Gear > Animation > Upgrade Selected Animator Controller. Without a clip in Ride the rider stands on the mount with arms out.

The Ride clip is a humanoid clip of your character, not something from the horse. Where to get one:

  • Mixamo (humanoid only; it has no horse or other animal motions): a sitting pose such as Sitting / Sitting Idle. Look for one with the knees apart; with the knees together the legs go through the horse. Download FBX for Unity, Without Skin.
  • Your own pose: in Blender, pose the character straddling (hips down, thighs angled down and out, knees bent, hands forward on the reins) and export a one-frame clip.
  • A horse pack with rider animations: some ship matching humanoid rider clips (idle, walk, gallop).

Setting it up: drop the FBX in Art/Animations/ (imports as Humanoid). On its Rig tab, Avatar Definition Create From This Model, Apply; if Unity says it isn't in T-pose, Configure > Pose > Enforce T-Pose, Done, Apply. On the Animation tab tick Loop Time (it's only automatic when the clip's name contains "ride"), Apply. Drag the clip (named after its take, e.g. mixamo.com) into the controller's Ride state. Then set RiderSeat's height and check the facing (above).

5. Making a pet#

A pet is an NPC that follows its owner instead of wandering.

  1. NpcTemplates > new row (or Tools > Axiom > Create > NPC…, copy Camp Dog):
Field Value Why
Name Fox Kit Shown over it
ModelType NPC_Fox Its prefab key
MaxHealth 0 0 = can't be attacked. Give it health and it can be
Title (empty) Empty shows "\<owner>'s companion"
LootTableId, XpReward, AggroRadius 0 It never fights
  1. Items > the item that summons it, with an Effects row Kind = Summon Pet, RefId = the NPC's Id, and an ItemUses row (EffectType 5, EffectValue = NPC Id) for the cooldown. Or Create > Item, copy Dog Whistle.
  2. Its buff: section 6.
  3. Commit, restart, Update Game Data.

Pet models and animations are NPC models: CHARACTER_ANIMATION_README.md part 7.

6. Pet buffs#

A pet's buff is a status put on its owner for as long as the pet is out: no timer, gone the moment the pet goes, and dispels don't remove it. What it does is the status's stat modifiers, so every pet can give something different.

  1. Statuses (Combat) > new row:
Field Example
Name Fox Cunning
Description Your fox keeps you sharp: critical chance +2%.
IconName Status/blessing
IsDebuff 0
DurationMs 60000 (must be positive; ignored for pet buffs)
  1. On that status, Stat modifiers > add a row: Stat = CritChance, Flat = 2. Several rows = several stats.
  2. Pet Buffs (NPCs category) > new row: NpcTemplateId = the pet, StatusId = the status. Or on the NPC itself, the Pet buffs (while summoned) section. Several rows = several buffs.
  3. Commit, restart.

Summoning shows "Fox Kit joins you: Fox Cunning.", and the buff appears on the buff bar with "No time limit" in its tooltip.

Which stats (Stat in StatusModifiers; Flat adds, Percent multiplies):

Stat Stat
Strength 0, Agility 1, Vitality 2, Intelligence 3, Spirit 4 primary MaxHealth 5, MaxMana 6
AttackPower 7, SpellPower 8, HealingPower 25 power Armor 9, Resist* 14-19 defence
CritChance 10 (%) , Haste 11 (%) HealthRegen 12, ManaRegen 13 per regen tick
DamageDonePct 20, DamageTakenPct 21, HealingDonePct 22, ThreatPct 23 % MoveSpeedPct 24 % movement speed

Keep pet buffs small: a pet is always out, so its buff is effectively permanent.

8. Rules and limits#

Rule Where Default
No summoning a mount in combat CombatRules MountInCombat (1 = allowed) 0: only OutOfCombatMs (6 s) after the last hit
Mounts in a dungeon Dungeons AllowMounts off
Flying in a dungeon never
Pet catches up CombatRules PetTeleportDistance (metres; 0 = never) 30: further than this, the pet blinks to just behind its owner
One pet at a time using any pet item while a pet is out sends it away; use the other item again to summon that one
Mount and pet saved over logout no you log in on foot, without a pet

The server checks every Mounts row at startup and skips (with a warning in its log) one with an empty ModelType, a SpeedMultiplier outside 1-4, a FlySpeedMultiplier outside 0-5 or a FlyVerticalSpeed outside 0-30. A PetBuffs row naming a missing NPC or status is skipped the same way.

9. Troubleshooting#

Symptom Fix
Holding Space doesn't take off The mount's CanFly is 0, you're in a dungeon, or the server wasn't restarted after the change
Rubber-banding while flying fast FlySpeedMultiplier changed but the server wasn't restarted (it allows the old speed)
Mount invisible, rider floating No prefab for its ModelType: make one (section 4). The ride still works
Rider in the wrong place on the mount Add or move the RiderSeat child
Mount doesn't animate Its Animator needs a controller with a Speed (and Flying) parameter
Make World Prefab says "'Mount_X' already maps to ..." The key had a prefab already. Drag the new prefab (Art/Objects/Mounts) onto the catalog entry's Prefab slot and save (section 4). Don't run the tool again: it would make a second copy
Mount is huge or tiny FBX Model tab > Scale Factor, then delete and remake the prefab and its controller (section 4)
Rider stands on the mount, arms out No clip in the character controller's Ride state (section 4)
Rider floats above the saddle RiderSeat is where the rider's feet go: lower it by about the pose's hip height
Mount faces backwards (ride forward: you see its head) Model child Rotation Y 180, and negate its Position X and Z (section 4)
Rider stands up or "jumps off" when you jump while riding Update CharacterAnimator.cs and MountVisuals.cs (riding, the rider gets no Jump/Land triggers and the mount gets the Jump). For the horse to jump too, its controller needs a Jump trigger and state: make it again with Create Mount Animator Controller
In water the rider swims and the mount dives with X Update the client scripts and both shared DLLs (protocol 45): mounted, the mount swims on the surface and the rider stays seated. For a swim clip on the mount, its controller needs a Swimming bool: make it again with Create Mount Animator Controller
Rider faces backwards only while mounted The Ride clip: its FBX > Animation > Root Transform Rotation > Bake Into Pose, Offset 180
Rider sits on the neck or the tail Move RiderSeat along Z; after turning the model, negate the model child's X/Z
Legs slide or run in place Change the Run threshold in the controller's Locomotion blend tree
"You can't summon a mount in combat." Wait 6 s after the last hit, or set MountInCombat 1
Pet gives no buff No PetBuffs row for its NPC id, or the status has no stat modifiers. Check the World server's log for a skipped PetBuffs row
Item has no name in game Hub > Tools > Update Game Data

10. Reference#

Mounts: Id, Name, ModelType, SpeedMultiplier, CanCarryPack, CanFly, FlySpeedMultiplier, FlyVerticalSpeed.

PetBuffs: Id, NpcTemplateId, StatusId, Sort.

Effects kinds: Mount = 4 (RefId = Mounts.Id), Summon Pet = 5 (RefId = NpcTemplates.Id).

For programmers: - Shared motor: LocomotionMode.Flying and StepFlying in Axiom.Movement/CharacterMotor.cs; MovementProfile.CanFly, FlySpeed, FlyVerticalSpeed; MoveInput.Pitch (nose up positive); MovementState.Flying (bit 4, reusing the retired gliding bit). - Server: MountManager (rules, flight per rider), MovementValidator (flight allowances only with Mount.CanFly), MovementStateRules (strips the Flying bit otherwise), PetManager (buffs as permanent statuses, StatusManager.Permanent; following across layers). - Client: MountVisuals (models, the mount's Animator), MovementSpeed (profile speeds and flight), LocalPlayerInput.FlightPitch (camera-steered flight), CharacterAnimator (Flying parameter). - Protocol 26: W2CMountState.CanFly, FlySpeedMultiplier, FlyVerticalSpeed.
