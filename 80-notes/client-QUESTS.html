Quests: objective types, stages, timers, repeatables and rewards#

Phase 3 of DATA_PHASES.md (protocol 30). A classic MMO quest system is a chain of "acts" with many kinds of trigger. Here that chain is flattened into objectives with stages.

Objective types (QuestObjectives.ObjectiveType)#

# Type TargetId Notes
1 Kill NPC template
2 Collect item Counted from the bag; taken at hand-in.
3 Talk NPC template
4 Craft recipe (0 = any) With TargetId 0, Param = the item made.
5 Use Item item
6 Use Skill skill Param = only on this NPC template (0 = anywhere).
7 Enter Area QuestAreas.Id Checked once a second.
8 Interact doodad template Any of its actions succeeding.
9 Kill Group QuestMonsterGroups.Id Any NPC in the group counts.
10 Kill In Zone — TargetKey = zone key; Param = lowest NPC level that counts.
11 Reach Level the level
12 Complete Quest quest Handing that quest in.
13 Gather item What you harvest, not what you hold.
14 Proficiency Rank profession RequiredCount = the rank.

RequiredCount is how many. Text is the log line; when it's empty a line is generated.

Stages and hidden objectives#

Stage is 1, 2, 3... A value of 0 counts as 1. - A stage's objectives only start counting once every objective of the earlier stages is done. - The log shows later-stage objectives greyed out with "(later)". - Hidden objectives aren't shown at all until their stage starts. Use this for "...and then the real target shows up".

Quest rules (Quests columns)#

Column Meaning
TimeLimitSeconds Finish within this long of accepting or the quest fails (it is dropped from the log with a message). The tracker and log show the countdown.
Repeatable, RepeatResetHours Can be taken again after hand-in, once the period ends. 24 = daily, 168 = weekly. Periods line up on CombatRules.DungeonResetHourUtc (weekly ones on Mondays), the same moment for everyone. The giver shows "!" again when it's available.
StartAreaId The quest is accepted automatically when you walk into that QuestAreas circle (if you could accept it).
StartItemId Using this item accepts the quest (a letter, a torn map).
AutoComplete Hands itself in the moment its objectives are done; no NPC needed. With a choice reward, the first choice is given.
CategoryId QuestCategories; the log groups quests by category.

Rewards (QuestRewards)#

The old RewardGold / RewardXp / RewardItemId columns still work. QuestRewards adds any number of lines:

Kind RefId Count
1 Item item how many (GradeId = the grade it comes at, 0 = base)
2 Gold — copper
3 XP — amount
4 Honor — amount
5 Status status template —

Rows with Choice = 1 are "choose one of". - The hand-in window shows them as buttons, and Complete stays disabled until you pick one. - The client sends the chosen row's index in the quest's reward list (C2WQuestComplete.RewardChoice). - The server re-checks the pick and checks that everything fits in your bag before taking anything.

Storage#

No database change on the persistence side. The quest's existing Progress string (128 characters max) carries the extras: "3,1|t=<accepted ms>|c=<last hand-in ms>|n=<times done>". Old rows (just the counts) load unchanged.

Map#

Areas of the Enter Area objectives you're currently on appear as pins on the map (QuestState.ActiveAreas).

Demo content (patch 063)#

  • Categories: Eastwatch, Daily, Dungeons. The existing quests 1–7 are filed under Eastwatch.
  • Fence Patrol (8, daily, Quartermaster Bren):
  • Stage 1: walk the Meadow Fence area.
  • Stage 2 (hidden until then): kill 4 Meadow Wolves.
  • Rewards: 40 gold and 120 XP, plus a choice of 3 Minor Health Potions or a grade-2 Copper Ring.
  • The Chieftain's Den (9, level 5, 30-minute limit):
  • Stage 1: kill 4 Warren orcs (Kill Group: Orc Grunt and Orc Shaman).
  • Stage 2 (hidden): kill the Warren Chieftain.
  • Reward: a choice of a grade-3 Fine Iron Sword or Wooden Buckler.

Code#

  • Core/Managers/QuestManager.cs: validation, events, stages, the 1 s sweep, repeat periods, hand-in.
  • Core/Managers/QuestProgress.cs: the progress string.
  • Client: QuestState helpers, QuestDialogueUI (choice rewards), QuestLogWindow (categories, timers), QuestTrackerUI.
