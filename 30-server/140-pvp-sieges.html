Server Systems

Factions, PvP, Justice & Sieges#

Sandbox world PvP is about factions fighting over contested land, crime and justice inside a faction, and guild sieges over castles. Axiom implements all three.

Piece File Job
FactionCatalog Core/Pvp/FactionCatalog.cs Factions, relations, territories, conflicts.
ZoneStateService Core/Pvp/ZoneStateService.cs The PvP mode in force at a spot now (painted mode + festivals + sieges).
PvpService Core/Pvp/PvpService.cs Who may attack whom; crime, honor, infamy, wanted.
JusticeService Core/Pvp/JusticeService.cs Arrest, jury trial, jail, outlaws.
TerritoryService (+ .Cycle, .Rosters, .Battle, .Castle, .Claim) Core/Pvp Territories, taxes, treasury, the weekly siege, the Lodestone claim.
ShardStateStore Core/Pvp/ShardStateStore.cs Territory and siege state in Persistence shard_state, surviving restarts.

World:AllowPlayerVersusPlayer must be true for any player-vs-player damage.

Factions#

Factions: Name, Description, Kind (0 player, 1 NPC), IsPlayable (offered at character creation), IsOutlaw (the Pirates: guilty outlaws join it), Color, HuntsCriminals (its guards attack Wanted players), GuardFactionId, HomeX/HomeZ.

Stances (FactionRelations: FactionA, FactionB, Stance 0 Hostile...): a row for the pair decides; otherwise the same faction is Friendly; otherwise Neutral. NPC faction 0 = monsters, hostile to everyone. Territory guards (NpcTemplates.FactionId = -1) take the owner's guard faction and change sides with it.

Who may attack a player#

Checked for both positions; the safer spot wins, so nobody can be shot into or out of a town.

Zone mode now Enemies Own/friendly faction
Safe (towns, respawn safe radii) never never
Peaceful (peace zone) never never
Festival (scheduled ZoneFestivals) never; faction NPCs neutral never
Contested (hostile zone) freely only with /bloodlust on, and it's a crime (unless they're Wanted)
War (siege, Lodestone event, war zone) freely, no crime freely, no crime

Always: never your party, raid or guild; never anyone in jail. Inside a siege's battle area the rosters decide, whatever the factions.

NPCs: an aggressive NPC attacks players whose faction is Hostile to its own (even in peace zones); players can't attack NPCs of a faction Friendly to theirs.

Crime, honor, infamy#

  • Crime points: attacking or killing your own side, theft. Fade while online. At WantedCrimePoints you're Wanted: guards that hunt criminals attack you, and dying puts you on trial.
  • Infamy: never fades.
  • Honor: killing an enemy player (not the same one twice within HonorRepeatMinutes), jury service, events. Spent at honor currency shops.
  • Saved in character_pvp; sent as W2CPvpSelf (yours) and W2CPlayerPvp (others' faction and flags). C2WPvpCommand toggles bloodlust. Lua: OnPvpKill, OnCrime.

Trial and jail#

A Wanted player who dies wakes in the courthouse and stands trial. Up to JurySize online players of their faction are called (W2CJuryInvite, C2WJuryVote, W2CTrial), vote within TrialSeconds, and earn JuryHonor. Guilty unless more jurors said Innocent (no jury = guilty).

  • Innocent: half the crime points are forgiven.
  • Guilty: jail for crime × JailSecondsPerCrime (clamped JailMin..JailMax); crime paid off. With OutlawInfamy or more infamy the player becomes an outlaw (the IsOutlaw faction).
  • Jail is a radius: walk out and you're put back; you can't fight or be attacked. The sentence is saved; logging out during a trial is a guilty plea.

Territories#

Territories (one per zone key): DefaultOwnerFactionId, TaxPct, BlessingStatusId, castle position and radii, the siege schedule (SiegeDayOfWeek, SiegeHourUtc, SiegeMinutes), bidding window (BidOpenHours, BidCloseHours), RosterCap, PeaceHours, LockdownRadius, spawns, RespawnWaveSeconds, MaxCamps, and Lodestone claim settings.

The owner (a faction, and once taken a guild of it):

  • is guarded by territory guards of its faction;
  • collects TaxPct of every trade pack sold and housing tax paid there into its treasury (the guild leader withdraws at the seal; the treasury is written empty before the gold is given);
  • is blessed: its faction's players there get BlessingStatusId;
  • buys castle upgrades (CastleUpgrades tiers: wall health %, defender buff, guards at siege start) from the treasury.

The weekly siege#

Peace ─▶ Bidding (guild leaders bid gold for the attacker slot) ─▶ Preparation (highest bid attacks; others refunded by mail;
         rosters fill) ─▶ Active (SiegeMinutes) ─▶ Peace (PeaceHours protection)
  • Bids take gold from the guild leader at once and are written before anything else; losing bids are mailed back with a delivery key (no double refunds even after a crash).
  • Rosters: the attacking guild and the owner guild (or owner faction) join their sides; others apply as mercenaries and the side's leader accepts. Only rostered players may be inside the lockdown area.
  • Battle: gates and walls stand in rings (an inner ring can't be damaged while any outer gate stands). Attackers place camps (forward respawns, SiegeRole 5) and siege weapons (SiegeRole 4 + SiegeWeapons rows: range, damage to structures/players, splash) from kit items; defenders man cannons. The dead return in waves. With every gate down an attacker claims the seal (a channel any hit breaks) and the territory changes hands. If time runs out, the defenders hold.
  • Rewards to everyone on a roster by score (SiegeRewards). W2CSiegeState, W2CSiegeInfo (the siege window, C2WSiegeCommand), W2CSiegeScore.
  • Everything survives a restart (shard_state kinds territory, siege, siegebid).

The Lodestone claim#

How a territory with no lord gets its first: from ClaimCraftHours before the event, each guild leader may craft the territory's Lodestone pack (a trade pack, so it can be stolen). At ClaimDayOfWeek/ClaimHourUtc the territory is at war for ClaimMinutes and its Lodestone doodad (SiegeRole 6) wakes; a contender carrying a pack channels it (any hit breaks it); success gives the territory to that guild and mails every member LodestoneRewards.

Recipes#

A second playable faction#

UPDATE Factions SET IsPlayable = 1 WHERE Id = 2;                         -- Eastreach now selectable
INSERT OR REPLACE INTO FactionRelations (Id, FactionA, FactionB, Stance) VALUES (1, 1, 2, 0);   -- Westmark vs Eastreach: hostile
-- races allowed for the faction live in Races / creation rules (CharacterRaceRules); give it a home and respawn points:
INSERT OR REPLACE INTO SpawnPointTables (Id, Name, X, Y, Z, IsDefault, IsRespawnPoint, SafeRadius, FactionId)
VALUES (20, 'Eastwatch Square', 320.0, 0, 460.0, 1, 1, 40, 2);

Then paint zones for it (Contested between the two homelands) in Dev Tools ▸ Zones.

A territory with a weekly siege#

Add a Territories row for the zone key, place gate and wall doodads (SiegeRole, MaxHealth, SiegeRing) and the seal around CastleX/CastleZ, castle cannons as DoodadSpawns, and SiegeWeapons/SiegeRewards/CastleUpgrades rows. Content Studio validates the hour (0–23) and the stance rules. Read Sieges (note) and PvP and honor (note) for the design notes behind each number.
