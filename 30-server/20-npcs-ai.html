NPCs, Spawning & AI#

Piece File Job
NpcTemplate GameData/Npcs/NpcTemplate.cs (NpcTemplates) What an NPC is: model, level, health, damage, aggro, loot, AI profile, faction, title, greeting.
NpcSpawnerTable GameData/Npcs/NpcSpawnTable.cs (NpcSpawners) Where NPCs appear and how many, how they move, when they respawn.
SpawnManager Core/Npcs/SpawnManager.cs Loads spawners; activates a spawner's group when a player comes near, despawns it after 30 s with nobody around.
NpcAiManager Core/Npcs/NpcAiManager.cs Every tick: wander, chase, attack, leash home, spawner scans, respawns.
NpcCombatAi Core/Ai/NpcCombatAi.cs Which ability to use: data-driven priorities, targets, health windows, boss phases.
ThreatService, ThreatTable Core/Threat Who the NPC is angry at.
NavMeshService, NavAgent Core/Navigation Paths around walls and up stairs on the baked navmesh (Navigation).
NpcCollisionService Core/Npcs NPC bodies that block players and each other.
NpcEntity Core/Entities/NpcEntity.cs One live NPC: id, template, position, health, threat table, spawner.

Templates#

Key NpcTemplates columns (full list in the reference):

Column Meaning
Name, Title, Level, ModelType display; ModelType is a prefab key resolved on the client
MaxHealth 0 = can't be attacked (merchants, quest givers). Give health on purpose.
AggroRadius 0 = never aggressive; otherwise attacks players who come this close
AttackRange, AttackCooldownMs, AttackDamageMin/Max its basic attack
Armor, Resistance damage reduction (Combat)
Rank 0 normal, 1 elite, 2 boss (more XP; bosses ignore crowd control and have phases)
XpReward 0 = formula from CombatRules
LootTableId what it drops (Loot)
AiProfileId how it fights (AiProfiles: Brute / Caster / Healer, heal threshold, assist radius)
FactionId 0 = monster (hostile to all), a faction id, or −1 = territory guard (takes the owner's guard faction)
IsStationary never wanders (vendors, guards on post)
BlocksPlayers, BlocksNpcs, BlocksWhenDead collision rules
RespawnSeconds, InteractRange, Greeting

Spawners and activation#

A spawner row places Count NPCs of a template at random spots within Radius of (X,Y,Z), at least MinSpacing (2.5 m) apart and not on top of a player.

NPCs sleep until a player is near. SpawnManager.ScanSpawners runs once a second: a spawner whose area overlaps any player's interest becomes active and spawns its group; one with nobody near for 30 s (DespawnGrace) despawns it. A world full of camps costs nothing where nobody is.

Spawner column Meaning
MoveType 0 default (wander unless stationary), 1 idle, 2 wander (around its own spot, resting 3–9 s), 3 patrol (PathId)
WanderRadius 0 = 8 m
RespawnMinSeconds/MaxSeconds random respawn delay; both 0 = the template's RespawnSeconds (default 30)
ActiveFromHour/ToHour only up during these game hours
DungeonId 0 = open world; otherwise spawned once per instance of that dungeon, never respawned
Tag, Dormant names for dungeon scripts; dormant spawners wait for a SpawnTag action
PathId, PoolId patrol routes and rare-spawn pools (columns exist; the PatrolPaths/SpawnPools tables aren't in the database yet)

Place spawners in Unity with Dev Tools ▸ Spawn Points, or the NPC Create wizard.

The AI loop#

Each tick NpcAiManager.Tick() (stage NpcAi) runs every active NPC:

idle/wander ── player enters AggroRadius ──▶ chase (3.2 m/s) ──▶ in AttackRange: attack / cast
     ▲                                          │
     └──── arrived home ◀── return (no re-aggro) ◀── target lost, dead, > 25 m chase or leash
  • Wander: a new waypoint at most every 300 ms (a decision, not a movement), within the wander radius, never more than 15 m (LeashRadius) from spawn.
  • Chase: towards the top of the threat table; on the navmesh when one is loaded (NavAgent), otherwise straight with terrain ground snap.
  • Leash/evade: too far from home, or the target unreachable on the navmesh for NpcUnreachableEvadeSeconds, and it walks home, resetting health and threat.
  • Unstuck: an NPC that hasn't moved for 700 ms while trying is ghosted through obstacles briefly.
  • Movement goes out through the snapshot dispatcher like players' (no per-NPC packets), so distant NPCs cost less bandwidth.

Threat#

ThreatService turns events into threat (Combat):

Event Threat
damage to the NPC damage × ThreatPerDamage
healing a player the NPC is fighting heal × ThreatPerHeal, split across those NPCs
taunt top of the table, fixated for TauntDurationMs
walking into aggro range AggroThreat

All scaled by the source's ThreatPct stat (a tank stance is a status with +100 %). The NPC switches target only when someone beats the current one by a margin, so it doesn't flicker.

Combat AI (abilities and phases)#

There is one AI for mobs and bosses. A boss is an NPC with phases and more ability rows.

  • AiProfiles: Role (Brute closes to melee; Caster/Healer stand off at their longest spell's range), HealBelowPct, AssistRadius (nearby aggressive friends join the fight).
  • NpcAbilities: each row is a Skills row (usually NpcOnly) with Priority (higher first), Target (0 current target, 1 self, 2 most hurt ally, 3 random enemy on its threat table), health window, Phase, Chance, CooldownMs, InitialDelayMs. Abilities cast through the same pipeline players use: cast bars, interrupts, effects, crowd control.
  • NpcPhases: below HealthBelowPct → enter Phase, Yell to everyone nearby, summon SummonCount × SummonTemplateId. Lua gets OnNpcPhase for anything more.

Death, loot and respawn#

CombatManager.KillNpc:

  1. Works out kill credit (who tagged it; party sharing within PartyShareRange).
  2. Stops casts, clears statuses, raises the NpcDied event (world events and achievements listen), clears threat.
  3. NpcAiManager.KillNpc: despawn for everyone (W2CNpcDespawn), schedule one replacement in RespawnSeconds via the Scheduler. If the spawner went inactive meanwhile, its next activation refills the group.
  4. LootManager.CreateCorpse: a lootable corpse for the eligible looters (Loot).
  5. XP to everyone sharing (with a party bonus, at each member's own level) and Lua OnKill / quest kill credit.

Talking to NPCs#

Right-clicking an NPC runs its interaction actions (InteractableActions rows: talk, trade/shop, quests, mailbox, auction, cash shop, warehouse...) through Core/Interaction. The template's Greeting is the Talk text. Lua can answer OnInteract for custom dialogue:

-- Lua/Server/blacksmith.lua
Server:RegisterPlayerEvent(6, function(player, templateId, kind)   -- 6 = OnInteract
    if templateId == 90010 then
        player:SendDialogue("Brannoc", "Bring me 10 Iron Ore and I'll make it worth your while.")
    end
end)

A new monster camp (data only)#

-- SQL/patches/120_wolf_camp.sql
INSERT OR REPLACE INTO NpcTemplates
  (Id, Name, Level, ModelType, MaxHealth, AggroRadius, AttackRange, AttackCooldownMs,
   AttackDamageMin, AttackDamageMax, Armor, Resistance, Rank, LootTableId, AiProfileId, FactionId,
   IsStationary, InteractRange, RespawnSeconds, Title, Greeting, XpReward)
VALUES (90200, 'Grey Wolf', 6, 'NPC_WOLF', 420, 12, 2.2, 1800, 14, 22, 20, 5, 0, 7, 2, 0,
        0, 4, 45, '', '', 0);

INSERT OR REPLACE INTO NpcSpawners (Id, TemplateId, X, Y, Z, Count, Radius, DungeonId, MoveType, WanderRadius)
VALUES (5200, 90200, 812.0, 0, 1034.5, 5, 18, 0, 2, 10);

AiProfileId 2 is the "Pack" profile (assists friends within 10 m). ModelType 'NPC_WOLF' must exist in the client's prefab catalogue and should have an NPC collision profile. Restart the World server. Easier: Tools ▸ Axiom ▸ Create ▸ NPC… does all of this with validation (Create wizards).

A boss with phases#

INSERT OR REPLACE INTO NpcAbilities (Id, NpcTemplateId, SkillId, Priority, Target, MinHealthPct, MaxHealthPct, Phase, Chance, CooldownMs, InitialDelayMs)
VALUES (700, 90300, 101, 10, 0, 0, 100, 0, 100, 8000, 3000),   -- Orc Chop the tank every 8 s
       (701, 90300, 102, 20, 3, 0, 100, 2, 100, 15000, 0),     -- phase 2+: Firebolt a random enemy
       (702, 90300, 106, 30, 1, 0, 30, 0, 100, 60000, 0);      -- below 30 %: Blood Rage on itself (once a minute)

INSERT OR REPLACE INTO NpcPhases (Id, NpcTemplateId, Phase, HealthBelowPct, Yell, SummonTemplateId, SummonCount)
VALUES (80, 90300, 2, 60, 'You will burn!', 90301, 3);

Skills 101, 102 and 106 are shipped NpcOnly skills (Orc Chop, Firebolt, Blood Rage). Any Skills row with its Effects works (Combat); see Grukk the Warchief (template 19) in the shipped data for a complete boss.

A new AI behaviour (code)#

To add a target rule such as "lowest-health enemy":

  1. Add LowestHealthEnemy = 4 to NpcAbilityTarget (GameData/Npcs/NpcAi.cs) and to Assets/AxiomMMOKit/Editor/ContentStudio/Schema/ContentEnums.cs (Content Studio dropdown).
  2. In NpcCombatAi, where the target of an ability row is resolved, add the case: walk npc.Threat.Entries, resolve players with _players.TryGetPeer, pick the lowest session.Combat.Health in range.
  3. Use it in a row: Target = 4.

Keep AI decisions data-driven: a new column or enum value on NpcAbilities/AiProfiles serves every NPC, where a C# special case serves one.

Debugging NPCs#

Problem Check
NPC never appears Spawner position (Dev Tools ▸ Spawn Points), ActiveFromHour, a player close enough, [Spawn] log lines.
Can't attack it MaxHealth = 0, or its faction is friendly, or a Safe zone.
Walks through walls No navmesh for that area ([NavMesh] log), or NavMeshEnabled = false. Bake it (Navigation).
Falls through floors / floats NpcGroundSnap, missing terrain heightmap for that tile.
Players walk through it No collision profile for its ModelType (export NPC collision), or BlocksPlayers = 0.
Boss phases don't trigger NpcPhases thresholds; abilities' Phase values. GM C2WDebugNpcHealth (DEV) sets an NPC's health % to test.
