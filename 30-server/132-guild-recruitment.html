Guild Recruitment (Recruit/Search)#

Community ▸ Guild ▸ Recruit/Search lets any player (with or without a guild) browse recruitment posts and apply, and lets a guild's recruiters put up a post and manage applicants. Posts show the guild's level and its buffs (Buff Effect), so levelling a guild is also what makes it attractive to join.

Piece File Job
RecruitmentRegistry Core/Social/Recruitment/RecruitmentRegistry.cs Every post and applicant on the shard: one object for every zone host (ShardShared.Recruitment). Search, filters, paging, limits.
RecruitmentService Core/Social/Recruitment/RecruitmentService.cs Per host: commands, faction/level/limit checks, chat notices. The home host saves and expires posts.
GuildManager Core/Social/GuildManager.cs CanRecruit (leader or a rank with Invite), AddApplicant (Persistence AddMember, so an offline applicant still joins), GuildSeen / GuildGone hooks that keep posts current.
C2WGuildRecruitHandler Core/Packets/C2WHandlers/C2WMissionHandlers.cs C2WGuildRecruit (186).
Packets Shared/Axiom.Network/Shared/Packets/GuildRecruitPackets.cs GuildRecruitAction, W2CGuildRecruitList (187), W2CGuildApplicants (188).
Client Scripts/Gameplay/Social/GuildRecruitState.cs, UiCommunityView.cs, Art/Resources/UI/Social/GuildRecruit.uss The page, the post editor and the applicants list.

Searching#

GuildRecruitAction.Search carries the filters; the server answers with one page (GuildRecruitPageSize, default 5) of W2CGuildRecruitList.

  • Interests: PvE, PvP, Explore, Trade, Siege, Craft. A post must have all the ticked ones.
  • Level range of the guild, and a name/text search that also matches the message.
  • Sort: Newest, Level, Members or Name. Hide Full Guilds.
  • Only guilds of the searcher's faction are listed.

Each row: level medallion, guild name, General (leader), Members (n/cap), time left, the message, interest tags, the lowest character level it takes, Buff Effect, and Apply (with a short message) or Withdraw.

Posting and applying#

Recruiters are the leader and any rank with the Invite permission. They get Post Recruitment / Edit Recruitment Post (up to 120 characters, interests, minimum character level, 1, 3 or 7 days up to GuildRecruitMaxDays) and Manage Applicants (n) with Accept / Decline. Posting again restarts the timer and keeps the applicants; Take Down removes the post and declines what's pending.

A character can have GuildRecruitMaxApplications (3) open applications. Applying notifies the guild's online recruiters in chat. When a recruiter accepts, the applicant joins even if offline or on another zone host, and their other applications close. Decline, take-down, expiry and disband each tell the applicant in chat if they're online.

State#

Posts live in Persistence's shard_state table (kind guild_recruit, key = guild id, one row per guild including its applicants), saved by the home host. Expired posts are removed while running and at start.

Rules (CombatRules)#

Rule Default
GuildRecruitEnabled 1 0 = no posts or applications
GuildRecruitMaxDays 7 the longest a post runs
GuildRecruitMaxApplications 3 open applications per character
GuildRecruitMaxApplicants 50 pending applicants per guild
GuildRecruitPageSize 5 posts per page

Packets: 186, 187, 188. Feature note: Guild recruitment and buffs.

Recipes#

A new interest tag ("Fishing")#

Interests are the [Flags] GuildInterest enum in GuildRecruitPackets.cs (shared). Add Fishing = 64 and widen All to 127: RecruitmentRegistry masks every post and query with GuildInterest.All, so a bit outside it is silently dropped. Rebuild the DLL, add the chip in UiCommunityView's Recruit/Search page and post editor, and the filter works with no other server change (it matches "has all ticked bits").
