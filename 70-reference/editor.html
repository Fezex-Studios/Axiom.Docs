Dungeon Dungeons An instanced dungeon: every group that goes in gets its own copy of its mobs and boss. Set its rules (runs per day, a time limit), give it a region, place mobs and a boss, add an entrance and an exit, and press Create. Create/DungeonWizard.cs
Item Items Weapons, armour, potions, materials, quest items. Fill in the details, add stats if it can be worn, say where players get it, and press Create. Create/ItemWizard.cs
Loot Table LootTables What an NPC drops when it dies: a gold range and items with their chances (0.25 = one kill in four). Give it to one or more NPCs here. Create/LootTableWizard.cs
NPC NpcTemplates Monsters, guards, vendors, quest givers. Start from a copy of one that already works (it keeps its abilities), change what you need, put spawners in the world, and press Create. Create/NpcWizard.cs
Quest Quests A quest someone offers: what they say, what the player must do, what they get, and which quest comes before it. Create/QuestWizard.cs
Skill Skills An ability a class learns (or an NPC uses): its targeting, cast time, cooldown and cost, and the effects it lands. Create/SkillWizard.cs
World Event WorldEvents A world boss on a timer, or a rift: waves of monsters and then its master. Everyone who fights it nearby shares the reward. Create/WorldEventWizard.cs
World Object DoodadTemplates Gatherables (ore, herbs, trees), chests, crafting stations, doors. Copy one that works, rename it, give it a model, change what it gives and how long it takes, place it in the world, and press Create. Create/WorldObjectWizard.cs
