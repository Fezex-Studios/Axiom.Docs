A player who is (or was) online. Safe to keep: once they leave, IsOnline() is false and the getters return 0 / "" / false.

Events#

Register with Server:On("OnKill", function(player, npcTemplateId) ... end) (or Server:RegisterPlayerEvent(9, fn)). The arguments after the event name are what your handler gets. From Core/Lua/PlayerEvent.cs.

Event Id Handler arguments and meaning
OnConnect 1 (player) - entered the world, see PlayerSpawnManager
OnDisconnect 2 (player) - leaving; the player's getters still work during the hook, see PlayerManager.HandlePlayerDisconnected
OnLevelUp 3 (player, newLevel)
OnChat 4 (player, channel, message) - return false to stop the message being sent; see LuaEngine.FireChat
OnDeath 5 (player, killerNpcTemplateId) - the player was killed, see CombatManager.KillPlayer
OnInteract 6 (player, targetTemplateId, targetKind) - used an NPC or doodad
OnItemUse 7 (player, itemTemplateId, slot) - fired AFTER a successful use, see PlayerManager.TryUseItem
OnHarvest 8 (player, doodadTemplateId, itemTemplateId, quantity) - per item a doodad action gave (harvest, chest, crop), see DoodadManager
OnKill 9 (player, npcTemplateId) - after an NPC the player attacked dies, see CombatManager
OnEnterZone 10 (player, zoneId, zoneKey, previousZoneId) - crossed into a zone (0 = no zone), incl. on spawn, see PlayerManager.UpdateZone
OnDoodadUse 11 (player, doodadTemplateId, funcId, actionType) - after any doodad action succeeded, see DoodadManager
OnCraft 12 (player, recipeId, itemTemplateId, quantity) - after a craft finished, see CraftingManager (Phase 7)
OnTheft 13 (player, doodadTemplateId, ownerCharacterId) - took someone else's overripe crop (8.3), trade pack or cargo (Phase 9)
OnTradePackSold 14 (player, packItemId, outletId, gold) - delivered a trade pack (Phase 9)
OnTrade 15 (player, partnerName, itemsGiven, itemsReceived, goldGiven, goldReceived) - a player trade went through (Phase 9)
OnPvpKill 16 (player, victimName, victimFactionId, wasCrime) - killed another player (Phase 10)
OnCrime 17 (player, points, charge) - committed a crime (Phase 10)
OnSentenced 18 (player, guilty, jailSeconds, outlawed) - a trial's verdict (Phase 10)
OnTerritoryCaptured 19 (player, territoryId) - claimed a territory's seal in a siege (Phase 10)
OnWorldEvent 21 20 was OnShipSunk - ships are removed for now. Ids are stable for Lua; don't reuse it.
OnTreasure 22 (player, mapItemId) - dug up a treasure (Phase 11)
OnClimb 23 (player, doodadTemplateId) - climbed something (Phase 11)
OnItemObtained 24 (player, itemTemplateId, quantity) - an item went into the bag (Phase 11: collections)
OnJuryVote 25 (player, trialId) - served on a jury (Phase 11 achievements)
OnAchievement 26 (player, achievementId) - completed an achievement (Phase 11)
OnSkillUse 27 (player, skillId, targetNpcTemplateId) - an ability went off (0 = no NPC target); quests count it (Docs/QUESTS.md)
OnQuestComplete 28 (player, questId) - turned a quest in, see QuestManager (missions count it, Docs/MISSIONS.md)
OnXpGained 29 (player, amount, source) - XP actually awarded (after bonuses; XpSource as a number), see ProgressionService
OnLaborSpent 30 (player, amount) - labor points spent, see ProfessionService
